# Kaiber Pricing 2026: Free Trial, Plans, and Credit Breakdown

Kaiber is an AI video and animation tool popular with musicians and creators for turning images, prompts, and audio into stylized, motion-driven videos. In 2026 it runs on a subscription-plus-credits model. Here's the full breakdown of what each plan costs and how far the credits go.

## Pricing Plans at a Glance

| Plan | Monthly Cost (Annual) | Credits | Best For |
|------|----------------------|---------|----------|
| Explorer | ~$5 | Small monthly pool | Casual creators |
| Pro | ~$15 | Larger pool, no watermark | Regular creators |
| Artist/Unlimited | ~$30+ | High volume, priority | Professionals |

## How Kaiber's Credits Work

Kaiber charges credits per second of generated video, so longer clips and higher-quality modes cost more. Each plan includes a monthly credit allotment; heavier tiers add more credits, remove watermarks, unlock upscaling, and grant commercial rights. Annual billing reduces the monthly rate.

## Explorer Plan (~$5/month billed annually)

The entry tier gives a modest monthly credit pool for short stylized clips. It's the cheapest way to make real, non-trial videos, though output may carry limits.

**Best for:** Casual creators and testing styles.

## Pro Plan (~$15/month billed annually)

Pro expands credits, removes watermarks, and unlocks higher quality and upscaling. For creators producing music visuals or social animations regularly, it's the working tier.

**Best for:** Musicians and content creators with steady output.

## Artist / Unlimited (~$30+/month)

The top tier offers large or effectively unlimited generation, priority processing, and full commercial rights — built for professionals shipping video at volume.

**Best for:** Pro creators and studios.

Kaiber's differentiator is artistic, audio-reactive animation — a style favored for music videos over photorealistic generation.

## Is Kaiber Worth Paying For?

### Paid pays off when:
- You want watermark-free, commercial-ready clips
- Audio-reactive or stylized animation is your goal
- You make music or social visuals regularly

### Free/trial is enough when:
- You're only testing styles and motion
- Credit limits don't block your project

## Tips to Optimize Kaiber Costs

1. **Plan clips before generating** — credits burn per second
2. **Start at Explorer** and upgrade only when you hit caps
3. **Reuse successful prompts and styles** to avoid re-rolls
4. **Go annual** for the discount

## Bottom Line

Kaiber's pricing scales from a ~$5 Explorer tier to ~$30+ unlimited plans, all on a credit system. For stylized, music-driven animation it's distinctive and affordable at entry. Test the style first, then pick the tier that matches your monthly output.
