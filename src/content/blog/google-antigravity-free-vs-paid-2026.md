# Google Antigravity Free vs Paid 2026: What Do You Actually Get?

Google Antigravity is one of the only major AI coding tools with a genuinely usable free tier. But how much of the tool do you actually get for free — and is the paid upgrade worth it? Here's an honest breakdown of the free vs paid plans in October 2026.

## Free Plan (Individual): What's Included

The free Individual tier gives you:

- **Gemini 3 Pro access**: The same model as the paid plans, with daily request limits
- **Desktop app + CLI**: Both interfaces are fully available
- **Single-agent execution**: One subagent at a time (no parallel workstreams)
- **Background tasks**: Up to 2 scheduled background tasks
- **Multi-model support**: Limited access to Claude Sonnet 4.6 and GPT-OSS-120B
- **Voice commands**: Basic voice input included

The practical daily cap on the free tier is roughly **15–25 moderate coding tasks**. For occasional use, side projects, or evaluation, this is enough to get a real sense of what Antigravity can do.

What the free tier lacks:
- Parallel multi-agent execution (3+ simultaneous agents)
- Priority model routing
- Unlimited background scheduling
- AI credits for overage

## Where the Free Tier Falls Short

### Parallel agents are locked

Antigravity's most distinctive feature — running multiple subagents simultaneously — is limited to one agent at a time on the free plan. The core value proposition of "parallel execution" isn't fully available without paying.

For simple tasks (write a function, fix a bug, generate a test), one agent is fine. For large feature development or migration work where the parallel advantage matters, you hit the wall quickly.

### Daily request caps interrupt flow

The free tier's request cap isn't a hard daily limit that resets cleanly — it's a soft cap with throttling. On heavy days, you'll notice slower responses and declined requests in the afternoon, even if the morning worked fine. This is frustrating when you're mid-task.

### No overage credits

Free users can't purchase additional credits. When you hit your daily limit, you wait until the next day. Paid plans can buy $25/2,500 credits packs to continue working.

## Pro Plan ($19.99/month): The Professional Tier

Pro removes the daily cap on Gemini 3 Pro, enables full parallel multi-agent execution (up to 3 agents), includes unlimited background task scheduling, and adds 500 AI credits/month for overage.

The upgrade from free to Pro is meaningful if:
- You're using Antigravity as your primary AI coding tool daily
- You need parallel agents for complex feature development
- Request throttling on the free tier is interrupting your work
- You want reliable background scheduling for overnight tasks

At $19.99/month, Pro is priced at roughly the same level as Cursor, Claude Code, and Kiro.

## Ultra Plans ($99.99 and $199.99/month): For Power Users

Ultra 5x ($99.99/month) and Ultra 20x ($199.99/month) are for developers running Antigravity as a full-time development automation platform.

Ultra 5x adds:
- Up to 8 concurrent parallel agents
- Priority routing (lower latency at peak times)
- 3,000 AI credits/month
- Early access to new features

Ultra 20x adds:
- Up to 20 concurrent parallel agents
- Maximum priority routing
- 15,000 AI credits/month
- Dedicated support

Most individual developers will never need Ultra. The use case is teams running continuous, large-scale automated development workflows — not individuals doing normal coding work.

## Should You Upgrade?

**Stay on free if:**
- You're evaluating Antigravity before committing
- You use it for occasional side projects
- Daily request limits aren't a problem for your usage pattern
- You have another primary coding tool (Cursor, Copilot) and want Antigravity as a supplement

**Upgrade to Pro if:**
- Antigravity is your primary AI coding tool for professional work
- You need parallel agents or reliable background scheduling
- You're hitting daily request limits regularly
- You want overage credits for peak work periods

**Upgrade to Ultra only if:**
- You're running Antigravity for continuous automated development
- You need 8+ parallel agents routinely
- You're a solo developer spending 8+ hours/day in Antigravity
