Small businesses are attacked more often than large enterprises—and hit harder when breaches succeed. Without a dedicated IT department, the right cybersecurity stack becomes your first and last line of defense. In 2026, five tools consistently rise to the top for teams that need serious protection without enterprise budgets.

## Why Small Businesses Are Prime Targets

Cybercriminals increasingly target small businesses because they tend to have weaker defenses than large companies while still holding valuable customer data, payment information, and business credentials. The average cost of a data breach for a small business now exceeds $200,000—enough to close most operations permanently.

The good news: a focused stack of four or five well-chosen tools eliminates the majority of attack vectors at a fraction of enterprise pricing.

## The Core Small Business Security Stack

A solid baseline for any small business covers three areas:

- **Identity and credentials** — stolen passwords account for over 80% of breaches
- **Network security** — encrypting traffic and masking your business IP
- **Endpoint protection** — stopping malware before it spreads

Here are the tools that deliver the best results in each category for 2026.

## Password Managers

### 1Password — Best for Teams

[1Password](/tools/1password/) is the gold standard for business password management. The Teams plan costs **$2.99 per user per month** (billed annually) and includes shared vaults, admin controls, activity logs, and two-factor authentication enforcement across your entire organization.

What sets 1Password apart for business use is its admin dashboard. You can see who has access to which credentials, enforce security policies, and revoke access the moment an employee leaves. It also integrates with Okta, Azure AD, and other identity providers if you already use them.

**Pricing:**
- Personal: $2.99/mo
- Teams Starter: $19.95/mo (up to 10 users)
- Business: $7.99/user/mo

The Teams Starter plan is ideal for small businesses—it covers up to 10 users at a fixed monthly rate.

### Bitwarden — Best Free Option

[Bitwarden](/tools/bitwarden/) is an open-source password manager that offers a generous free tier and transparent security practices. For small businesses watching costs, the Teams Organization plan is **$4/user/month**—and the free tier works for individuals who just need basic password management.

Bitwarden's open-source nature means security researchers worldwide audit its code continuously. It supports all major browsers, mobile platforms, and even command-line access for developers. You can also self-host Bitwarden if data sovereignty is a concern.

**Pricing:**
- Free: unlimited passwords, one user
- Premium: $10/year per user
- Teams Organization: $4/user/mo

## VPN and Network Security

### NordVPN Teams — Best Value VPN for Business

NordVPN's business offering starts at **$3.49/month per user** on the Basic plan, making it one of the most affordable ways to encrypt your team's internet traffic. For employees working from coffee shops, coworking spaces, or home networks, a VPN prevents man-in-the-middle attacks and hides your business traffic from ISP logging.

NordVPN Teams also includes a dedicated IP option, which is useful if you whitelist IPs for internal tools or client-facing systems.

**Pricing:**
- Basic: $3.49/user/mo
- Advanced: $4.49/user/mo (adds threat protection and dedicated IP)
- Enterprise: custom

### Proton — Best Privacy-First Option

Proton (formerly ProtonMail/ProtonVPN) has expanded into a full privacy suite including email, VPN, cloud storage, and password management under one umbrella. The free tier includes encrypted email and a limited VPN, while **Proton Unlimited** at **$9.99/month** covers everything—email, 500GB storage, full VPN access, and Proton Pass (password manager).

For small businesses that prioritize end-to-end privacy—law firms, healthcare adjacent businesses, or anyone handling sensitive communications—Proton's integrated approach is worth considering. All services are based in Switzerland and operated under strict European privacy law.

**Pricing:**
- Free: encrypted email + limited VPN
- Mail Plus: $3.99/mo
- Proton Unlimited: $9.99/mo (all services)
- Business: $6.99/user/mo (email and storage focus)

## Endpoint Security

### CrowdStrike Falcon Go — Best Endpoint Protection

Endpoint detection and response (EDR) used to be exclusively enterprise territory. CrowdStrike's **Falcon Go** plan at **$59.99/month** (for up to 5 endpoints, billed annually) brings AI-powered threat detection to small businesses.

CrowdStrike's platform monitors device behavior in real time, stopping ransomware, malware, and fileless attacks before they execute. The lightweight agent runs in the background with minimal performance impact.

For businesses in regulated industries or handling sensitive client data, CrowdStrike's detection speed (measured in seconds, not hours) justifies its cost compared to traditional antivirus solutions that only catch known threats.

**Pricing:**
- Falcon Go: $59.99/mo (up to 5 devices)
- Falcon Pro: $99.99/mo (up to 5 devices, adds threat intelligence)
- Enterprise: custom pricing

## Implementation Priorities

If you're starting from scratch, roll out tools in this order:

1. **Password manager first** — Migrate all shared credentials into 1Password or Bitwarden before anything else. Credential theft is the #1 attack vector.
2. **Enable MFA everywhere** — Use your password manager's built-in TOTP or a dedicated authenticator app. Add this to email, cloud storage, and any SaaS tools immediately.
3. **Deploy VPN for remote workers** — Anyone accessing business systems from outside the office should be on a VPN.
4. **Add endpoint protection** — Once the above is in place, evaluate CrowdStrike or similar EDR for devices that touch sensitive data.

---

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- **Using personal accounts for business credentials** — Even if an employee uses a great personal password manager, you have no visibility or control. Use a team plan.
- **Skipping MFA because it's inconvenient** — A few seconds per login is worth the protection. Modern password managers make MFA seamless.
- **Treating cybersecurity as a one-time project** — Run quarterly access audits, remove credentials for departed employees, and review your tools annually as your team grows.

## Final Verdict

For most small businesses, the winning combination is **Bitwarden Teams** (cost-effective, open source) + **NordVPN Basic** (affordable network security) + **CrowdStrike Falcon Go** (genuine endpoint protection). Budget roughly $70-100/month for a 5-person team—far less than the cost of a single breach.

Larger teams or those in regulated industries should consider upgrading to **1Password Business** for its superior admin controls and **Proton Business** if end-to-end encrypted email is a compliance requirement.
