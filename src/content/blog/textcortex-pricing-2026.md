# TextCortex Pricing 2026: Free, Lite, and Unlimited Plans Breakdown

TextCortex is an AI writing assistant with a browser extension, a chatbot (Zeno), and knowledge-base features. In 2026 it positions itself as a lower-cost alternative to Jasper and Copy.ai. Here's exactly what it costs and what each tier includes.

## Pricing Plans at a Glance

| Plan | Monthly Cost (Annual) | Creations | Best For |
|------|----------------------|-----------|----------|
| Free | $0 | ~20/day | Light personal use |
| Lite | ~$5.59 | ~generous monthly pool | Individuals and freelancers |
| Unlimited | ~$17.49 | Unlimited creations | Regular content producers |
| Business | Custom | Team + admin | Organizations |

## How "Creations" Work

A creation is one AI output — a rewrite, a paragraph, a chatbot reply, or a generated draft. Lower tiers cap creations; the Unlimited plan removes the cap, which is TextCortex's main selling point against per-word or credit-limited rivals.

## Free Plan ($0)

The free tier gives a daily allowance of creations plus access to the extension and basic features. It's enough for occasional rewriting and short drafts.

**Best for:** Casual users who write with AI now and then.

## Lite Plan (~$5.59/month billed annually)

Lite is aggressively cheap. It raises your creation limit, unlocks more of the assistant's capabilities, and adds longer outputs. At under six dollars a month, it undercuts almost every mainstream competitor.

**Best for:** Freelancers and individuals on a tight budget.

## Unlimited Plan (~$17.49/month billed annually)

Unlimited removes creation caps entirely and adds premium models, knowledge bases (upload your own docs), and advanced customization. For anyone writing daily, unlimited output at this price is the headline value.

**Best for:** Bloggers, marketers, and content producers writing every day.

## Business Plan (Custom)

Business adds team seats, shared knowledge bases, centralized billing, and admin controls.

## How TextCortex Compares on Price

| Tool | Entry Paid Price | Model |
|------|-----------------|-------|
| TextCortex Lite | ~$5.59/mo | Cheapest entry, creation-limited |
| TextCortex Unlimited | ~$17.49/mo | Unlimited outputs |
| Jasper Creator | ~$39/mo | Marketing-focused, brand voice |
| Copy.ai | ~$36/mo | Workflows + GTM automation |
| Writesonic | ~$16/mo | SEO + article writing |

TextCortex's pitch is price plus an "unlimited" tier that most rivals don't offer, wrapped in a browser extension that works across the web.

## Is TextCortex Worth It?

### It's worth it when:
- Budget is a priority and you write regularly
- You want unlimited output without watching a credit meter
- A cross-web extension fits how you work

### Look elsewhere when:
- You need Jasper's marketing templates and brand-voice depth
- You want tightly integrated SEO scoring (Writesonic, Surfer)

## Tips to Optimize TextCortex Costs

1. **Start free** — the daily allowance reveals whether you need paid
2. **Jump to Unlimited if you write daily** — the flat cap removal beats rationing
3. **Use annual billing** — it lowers already-low prices further
4. **Load knowledge bases** — grounding outputs in your docs cuts editing time

## Bottom Line

TextCortex competes almost entirely on price and its unlimited tier. Lite at ~$5.59/month is one of the cheapest capable AI writers available, and Unlimited at ~$17.49/month removes the credit anxiety that plagues rivals. If brand-voice depth isn't your priority, it's a strong-value pick.
