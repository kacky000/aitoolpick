# Kling AI Pricing 2026: Complete Plan Breakdown

Kling AI has become one of the most popular AI video generation tools partly because of its competitive pricing. The free tier is genuinely useful, and paid plans undercut Western competitors significantly. Here's a complete breakdown of every Kling pricing plan for 2026.

## Kling AI Pricing Plans

| Plan | Monthly Price | Annual Price | Credits/Month |
|------|---------------|--------------|---------------|
| Free | $0 | — | 166 credits/day |
| Starter | ~$8/month | ~$66/year | 660 credits |
| Standard | ~$22/month | ~$180/year | 3,000 credits |
| Pro | ~$50/month | ~$410/year | 8,000 credits |
| Enterprise | Custom | Custom | Custom |

*Note: Prices shown in USD equivalent. Kling prices in CNY (Chinese Yuan); exact USD conversion fluctuates slightly.*

## Understanding the Credit System

Kling AI operates on a credit-based system. Different generation types consume different numbers of credits:

### Credit Costs by Generation Type

| Generation Type | Resolution | Duration | Credits |
|----------------|-----------|---------|---------|
| Text-to-video | 720p | 5 seconds | ~10 credits |
| Text-to-video | 1080p | 5 seconds | ~20 credits |
| Image-to-video | 720p | 5 seconds | ~10 credits |
| Pro model (Kling 2.0) | 1080p | 5 seconds | ~35 credits |
| Video extension | — | 5 seconds | ~10 credits |

### How Many Videos Can You Make Per Month?

| Plan | Standard 1080p 5s Videos | Budget 720p Videos |
|------|--------------------------|-------------------|
| Free (daily) | ~2-3/day | ~8/day |
| Starter | ~33/month | ~66/month |
| Standard | ~150/month | ~300/month |
| Pro | ~228/month | ~800/month |

The free tier's 166 daily credits — roughly 4,980 credits/month — is actually more than the paid Starter plan. The difference is that free credits expire daily (unused credits don't roll over), while paid plan credits roll over monthly.

## Free Tier: What's Included?

Kling's free tier is genuinely useful:

- **166 credits per day** (reset daily)
- Access to standard Kling models
- 720p and 1080p resolution options
- Text-to-video and image-to-video
- Video extension feature
- Watermark on free generations

**What the free tier lacks:**
- Priority queue access (paid users generate faster)
- Access to latest Pro models (Kling 2.0 requires credits or paid plan)
- Commercial usage rights (free generations are for personal use only)

## Is the Kling Paid Plan Worth It?

**Starter ($8/month)** is worth it if:
- You need commercial usage rights for your videos
- You want credits to roll over month-to-month
- You want priority generation queue access

**Standard ($22/month)** is worth it if:
- You're creating 50-150 videos/month
- You need reliable monthly capacity for client work

**Pro ($50/month)** is worth it if:
- You're running a video content agency or high-volume production
- You need API access for automated pipelines
- 228+ 1080p videos per month is your baseline need

## Hidden Costs to Know

1. **Model upgrades**: Using Kling 2.0 Pro model costs significantly more credits than the standard model
2. **Longer clips**: Each 5-second extension doubles credit cost linearly
3. **High resolution**: 1080p costs approximately 2x more than 720p
4. **Commercial license**: Free tier doesn't include commercial rights — factor this into your budget

## Annual vs Monthly Billing

Kling offers approximately 15-20% savings on annual billing vs monthly. If you're committed to AI video creation for the year, annual billing is worthwhile.

## How to Get Started for Free

1. Visit [klingai.com](https://klingai.com)
2. Create an account (Google account works)
3. You receive 166 credits daily automatically
4. Start generating with text-to-video or upload an image for image-to-video

No credit card required for the free tier.

## Verdict on Kling Pricing

Kling AI offers the most accessible AI video generation pricing in 2026. The free tier with daily credits is more generous than most competitors, and paid plans deliver significantly more video volume per dollar than Sora or Runway.

For individual creators and small teams, Kling's pricing makes professional AI video creation economically viable for the first time.
