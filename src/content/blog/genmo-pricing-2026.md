# Genmo Pricing 2026: Is the Open Video Model Free to Use?

Genmo is an AI video generator known for Mochi, its openly released text-to-video model. That open approach makes its pricing different from most rivals — you can use the hosted product or run the model yourself. In 2026, here's what it actually costs.

## Pricing at a Glance

| Option | Cost | What You Get | Best For |
|--------|------|--------------|----------|
| Free (Hosted) | $0 | Limited monthly credits | Testing |
| Paid (Hosted) | ~$10–30 | More credits, priority, HD | Creators |
| Self-Hosted (Open Model) | Compute only | Unlimited local generation | Developers |

## How Genmo's Pricing Works

Genmo offers a hosted web product on a credit system, plus an openly available model you can run on your own hardware. On the hosted side, generations consume credits based on length and resolution; self-hosting costs only your compute, with no per-video fee. That split appeals to both casual creators and developers.

## Free (Hosted, $0)

The free hosted tier gives a limited monthly credit allowance to generate short clips. It's the quickest way to try Genmo's output without any setup.

**Best for:** Testing quality with zero commitment.

## Paid (Hosted, ~$10–30/month)

Paid tiers expand credits, remove or reduce limits, add priority generation, and unlock higher resolution. For creators who want convenience without managing infrastructure, this is the practical route.

**Best for:** Regular creators who prefer a hosted tool.

## Self-Hosted (Open Model)

Because Genmo released Mochi openly, developers with capable GPUs can run generation locally — paying only for compute and gaining unlimited, private output. This is unique among mainstream video generators.

**Best for:** Developers and privacy-focused teams.

## Is Genmo Worth It?

### Genmo fits when:

## Tips to Optimize Genmo Costs

1. **Self-host if you have a GPU** — unlimited generation for compute cost
2. **Use free hosted credits** to judge quality first
3. **Keep clips short** on the hosted plan to save credits
4. **Batch local runs** to maximize hardware use

## Bottom Line

Genmo's pricing is uniquely flexible: free hosted credits, affordable paid tiers, or self-hosting the open model for just compute. If you value openness or want to avoid per-video fees, it stands apart from closed competitors. Try the free tier, then decide between hosted and self-hosted.
