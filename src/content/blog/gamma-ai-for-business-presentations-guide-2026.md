## What Is Gamma AI?

Gamma is an AI-powered presentation tool that generates complete decks from a text prompt or outline. Unlike Canva or PowerPoint — where you drag and arrange elements manually — Gamma treats your presentation as a **living document**: responsive, shareable as a link, and editable with AI assistance throughout the process.

The output is not just slides — Gamma creates "cards" (their term for slides) that look great in a browser, can be shared as a link (no download required), embedded in a webpage, or exported as a PDF or PowerPoint file.

## How to Get Started

**Step 1**: Go to gamma.app and create a free account.

**Step 2**: Click "New" and choose one of three creation modes:
- **Generate from prompt**: Describe your presentation in plain English
- **Import from outline**: Paste your bullet points and let Gamma structure them
- **Start from template**: Choose a pre-built template for your use case

**Step 3**: Review and refine. Gamma generates a complete deck in 30–60 seconds. Every card is editable, and you can use the AI panel to rephrase, expand, or redesign any section.

## Core Features for Business Users

### AI Generation

The generation prompt supports natural instructions: "Create a 10-slide investor pitch for a B2B SaaS company targeting mid-market HR teams. Include a market size slide and a competitive landscape." Gamma follows the structure, chooses appropriate layouts, and suggests imagery.

### AI Edit Panel

Select any card and open the AI panel to:
- Rephrase text in a different tone
- Expand a section with more detail
- Condense long text into bullet points
- Suggest a different layout

### Smart Templates

Gamma ships with templates designed for specific business scenarios:
- Sales decks and one-pagers
- Quarterly business reviews (QBRs)
- Product roadmaps
- Job-to-be-done frameworks
- Investor pitches
- Marketing campaign proposals

### Responsive Layouts

Each card automatically adapts its layout based on content length. You don't fight with text boxes overflowing their containers — Gamma reflows content intelligently.

### Sharing and Analytics

Instead of attaching a PDF, share your Gamma deck as a link. You can see who viewed it, for how long, and which slides got the most attention. This is genuinely useful for sales follow-ups.

## Gamma for Common Business Scenarios

### Sales Decks

Gamma's strength is getting a first draft in front of a prospect fast. Use the generation prompt to create a deck tailored to a specific company, then manually adjust the case study and pricing slides. The interactive link format means prospects can scroll at their own pace rather than sitting through a screen share.

### Internal Presentations (All-Hands, QBRs)

Generate a skeleton from your notes, then use the AI to expand each section. Gamma's clean default theme looks professional without any design effort. For QBRs, the data visualization cards (charts, metrics) render cleanly without requiring Excel exports.

### Training Materials

Gamma creates scrollable training documents that work like presentations but feel like web pages. Teams at companies like Notion and Linear use this format for internal onboarding guides.

### Client Proposals

The PDF export is clean enough to attach to emails or print. For high-stakes proposals, use Gamma to generate the structure and flesh it out, then export to PowerPoint for final polishing if needed.

## Tips for Better Gamma Presentations

**1. Write a detailed prompt.** The more specific you are about audience, tone, and structure, the better the first draft. Include the number of slides you want.

**2. Lock your theme first.** Change colors and fonts before editing content — reformatting after content is added takes longer.

**3. Use the AI to trim, not just expand.** If a slide is too text-heavy, highlight and ask Gamma to condense it to three bullet points.

**4. Replace stock images.** Gamma auto-selects images from Unsplash, but they're generic. Replace hero images with product screenshots or actual photos for higher impact.

**5. Share the link, not the PDF.** The interactive format gets significantly more engagement than attached PDFs, especially for sales decks.

## Limitations to Know

- Design control is more limited than PowerPoint or Figma — you can't pixel-perfect adjust every element
- Complex data visualizations require an external tool (Tableau, Google Sheets) before embedding
- The free tier's 400-credit limit runs out fast for power users
- Templates are less diverse than Canva's library
