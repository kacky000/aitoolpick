Descript has made audio and video editing approachable for people who have never used a traditional editing tool. Instead of cutting waveforms and scrubbing through timelines, you edit a transcript — and the media follows. This guide walks you through everything you need to start using Descript confidently in 2026.

## What Is Descript?

Descript is an AI-powered audio and video editor that transcribes your recordings automatically and lets you **edit by editing the text transcript**. Delete a sentence from the transcript, and the corresponding audio and video disappears. It sounds simple — and it is — but the implications are profound for podcasters and content creators.

Beyond transcript-based editing, Descript includes:
- **Overdub**: Clone your voice and regenerate mispronounced words in AI audio
- **Studio Sound**: One-click noise removal and audio enhancement
- **Remove filler words**: Automatically delete "um," "uh," and false starts
- **Screen recording**: Record and edit screencasts
- **Video editing**: Multi-track video timeline with captions, clips, and templates
- **Collaboration**: Share projects and invite collaborators

## Getting Started: Your First Project

**Step 1: Create an account**

Go to descript.com and create a free account. The free tier allows 1 hour of transcription per month.

**Step 2: Start a new project**

Click "New Project" and choose:
- **Record**: Use Descript to record directly (audio or screen)
- **Import**: Upload a file from your computer or from a cloud storage service

**Step 3: Auto-transcription**

Descript will transcribe your file automatically. For English content, accuracy is typically 90–95%. Speaker labels are auto-assigned — review and rename them to make editing cleaner.

**Step 4: Edit the transcript**

Read through the transcript like a document. To remove a section:
- Highlight the text you want to cut
- Press Delete

That's it. The audio and video is cut in sync with the text.

## Core Features Explained

### Removing Filler Words

This is Descript's most-loved feature. After transcription, go to **Actions → Remove filler words**. Descript shows you every "um," "uh," "like," and false start in your recording, lets you preview them, and removes them with one click.

A 30-minute raw podcast recording can be cleaned of filler words in under 5 minutes.

### Studio Sound

Under **Audio Enhancement**, toggle on Studio Sound. This uses AI to:
- Remove background noise (fans, AC, street noise)
- Balance levels across speakers
- Reduce reverb in room recordings

The difference on a home recording is dramatic. It doesn't replace a professional microphone, but it gets you significantly closer to studio quality.

### Overdub (Voice Cloning)

If you misspoke a word or want to insert new audio without re-recording, Overdub lets you type the correction and Descript generates it in your cloned voice. 

To set up Overdub: Go to your profile settings, record 10 minutes of your voice reading provided passages, and wait for your voice model to train (usually 30–60 minutes). Once ready, you can overdub any word or sentence.

**Important**: Overdub is available on the Creator plan and above.

### Multi-Speaker Transcription

Descript handles interviews, panels, and multi-guest podcasts well. It auto-detects speakers and assigns speaker labels. After reviewing the transcript, correct any mis-labeled speakers once — Descript learns and corrects throughout.

### Captions and Clips

For social media repurposing:
- **Captions**: Auto-generated captions with styling options (for Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts)
- **Clips**: Export short segments directly to social formats

This alone saves hours per week for content creators who repurpose long-form content.

## Descript for Different Creators

### Podcasters

Descript is built for podcasters. Import your raw recording, remove filler words, cut the boring parts, add intro/outro music, export to MP3. A 45-minute episode goes from raw to publish-ready in about 20 minutes.

### YouTubers

The video editing workflow is strong for talking-head and interview content. Upload a video, edit the transcript, add B-roll from your drive, add captions for YouTube Shorts repurposing, export. For heavily-edited cinematic content, DaVinci Resolve is more powerful — but for 80% of YouTube content, Descript handles it faster.

### Course Creators

Record your lessons, clean them with Studio Sound, use the transcript as auto-generated notes, clip key segments for social. Descript integrates with Loom and Riverside for courses recorded with multiple speakers.

### Business Teams

Internal video updates, Loom-style messages, and knowledge base recordings all benefit from Descript. Teams use it to create searchable, transcribed video libraries.

## Tips for Beginners

**1. Always clean up speaker labels first.** Before editing, fix the speaker labels — it makes the transcript much easier to read and edit.

**2. Use filler word removal before manual editing.** It takes 30 seconds and removes the most obvious issues automatically.

**3. Save your projects to the cloud.** Descript projects can get large — let their cloud sync handle backup.

**4. Export a rough cut first.** Before final polish, export a rough draft and listen to it. You'll catch edits that look wrong in the transcript but sound right, and vice versa.

**5. Explore the template gallery.** Descript has show templates (podcast, YouTube, webinar) that pre-configure your export settings correctly.
