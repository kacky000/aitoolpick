# Opus Clip Pricing 2026: Free, Starter, and Pro Plans Breakdown

Opus Clip turns long videos into short, captioned clips for TikTok, Reels, and Shorts using AI. In 2026 its pricing is metered by upload minutes, and the difference between tiers comes down to volume and watermarks. Here's the full breakdown.

## Pricing Plans at a Glance

| Plan | Monthly Cost | Upload Minutes | Watermark |
|------|-------------|----------------|-----------|
| Free | $0 | ~60 min/mo | Yes |
| Starter | ~$9 (annual) | ~150 min/mo | No |
| Pro | ~$19 (annual) | ~300 min/mo | No |
| Business | Custom | High volume + team | No |

## How "Upload Minutes" Work

Opus Clip bills by the length of the source video you upload, not the number of clips produced. Upload a 30-minute podcast and it deducts 30 minutes from your monthly allowance — regardless of how many short clips the AI generates from it. This is the number to watch when choosing a tier.

## Free Plan ($0)

The free tier gives you around 60 upload minutes per month with an Opus watermark on exported clips. It's ideal for testing the AI's clip selection and caption quality before paying.

**Best for:** Creators evaluating the tool or posting very occasionally.

## Starter Plan (~$9/month billed annually)

Starter removes the watermark and roughly doubles your monthly minutes. For a solo creator repurposing one or two long videos a week, this is usually enough.

**Best for:** Individual creators with a steady but modest publishing cadence.

## Pro Plan (~$19/month billed annually)

Pro roughly doubles Starter's minutes again and unlocks the full feature set — advanced editing, brand templates, and priority processing. This is the tier for creators and small teams shipping short-form daily.

**Best for:** Active creators and social media managers repurposing content daily.

## Business Plan (Custom)

Business adds high-volume minute allotments, multiple seats, and team collaboration. Pricing scales with usage and seats.

Opus Clip's niche is narrow and strong: automatically finding the best moments in long video and captioning them. It's a repurposing engine, not a full editor.

## Is Opus Clip Worth Paying For?

### Paid pays off when:
- You publish short-form clips regularly and the watermark hurts your brand
- Manual clipping eats hours you'd rather spend creating
- You consistently exceed 60 source minutes a month

### Free is enough when:
- You post short clips rarely
- A watermark doesn't bother you

## Tips to Optimize Opus Clip Costs

1. **Count source minutes, not clips** — that's what's billed
2. **Choose annual billing** — it meaningfully lowers the monthly rate
3. **Batch uploads** — plan your month's videos against your minute allowance
4. **Start free** — confirm the AI picks good moments for your content style

## Bottom Line

Opus Clip is priced for creators, not studios. Starter at ~$9/month annually removes the watermark and covers a typical solo cadence; Pro suits daily short-form output. Test the free tier first to see whether the AI's clip choices match your voice.
