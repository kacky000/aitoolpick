OpenAI launched Presence on July 22, 2026 — an enterprise platform for deploying AI-powered voice and chat agents. It's not a self-serve API. It's closer to a managed service: OpenAI engineers help you build and deploy the agent, you pay custom pricing, and you're locked into OpenAI's stack.

Here's what Presence actually is, what it costs (as much as is known), and whether it's a fit for your organization.

## What Is OpenAI Presence?

Presence is OpenAI's answer to the enterprise AI agent market. It targets companies that want to replace or augment customer-facing voice and chat workflows with AI — think call center automation, interactive voice response (IVR) replacement, and web chat agents.

Unlike ChatGPT Enterprise (which gives employees an AI assistant), Presence is designed to **face customers**, not employees.

Key capabilities:
- **Real-time voice agents** — AI phone agents handling inbound and outbound calls
- **Chat agents** — Web and messaging chatbot deployments
- **Multi-turn context** — Maintains context across long conversations without losing state
- **Workflow integration** — Connects to CRM, ticketing, and backend systems
- **Forward Deployed Engineers** — OpenAI's own engineers do the implementation

## How It Works

Presence is not a product you sign up for and configure yourself. The deployment model works like this:

1. You contact OpenAI's sales team and describe your use case
2. OpenAI scopes the engagement (this is where pricing is negotiated)
3. OpenAI's Forward Deployed Engineers build and configure the agent
4. You launch with ongoing support from OpenAI

This is a **consulting-adjacent model** more than a traditional SaaS product. OpenAI handles the complexity; you pay for the outcome.

Selected systems integrators can also deploy Presence on your behalf, giving you a third-party option for organizations that don't want to work directly with OpenAI.

## Pricing

OpenAI has **not released public pricing** for Presence. Each engagement is scoped individually based on:
- Call volume and concurrent agent requirements
- Complexity of workflow integrations
- Compliance and data handling requirements
- Level of ongoing support needed

From available information, Presence is positioned at **enterprise-level pricing** — not accessible for startups or SMBs. Budget expectations from similar managed AI services suggest $50,000+ minimum engagements, but this is unconfirmed.

"Boots on the ground prices" is how The Register described the model — meaning you're paying for OpenAI's team time in addition to compute costs.

## Who Is Presence For?

**Strong fit:**
- Large enterprises currently running legacy IVR systems that want AI voice agent replacement
- Companies with high call volumes (contact centers, financial services, healthcare intake)
- Organizations that don't have engineering teams to build and maintain AI agent infrastructure
- Regulated industries where having OpenAI's engineers directly involved in deployment reduces compliance risk

**Not a fit:**
- Startups or growth-stage companies (pricing model is enterprise-oriented)
- Teams with engineering capacity who want to control their own stack
- Organizations that need self-serve onboarding or transparent pricing upfront

## Presence vs Building Your Own Stack

The alternative to Presence is assembling your own AI agent infrastructure using tools like Vapi, Retell AI, or custom deployments on top of the OpenAI Realtime API.

| | OpenAI Presence | DIY Voice Agent Stack |
|--|-----------------|----------------------|
| **Cost** | Custom (high) | Variable (pay per use) |
| **Control** | Low | High |
| **Setup time** | Weeks-months (OpenAI deploys) | Weeks (your team builds) |
| **Engineering required** | None (OpenAI provides) | Significant |
| **Customization** | Limited to OpenAI's stack | Full control |
| **Vendor lock-in** | High | Moderate |
| **Compliance support** | Included in engagement | DIY |

For enterprises without an engineering team and with a clear voice agent use case, Presence removes substantial complexity. For any team that wants control over costs, model choices, or integration points, building on Vapi or Retell AI is likely cheaper and more flexible.

## Key Concerns

**Vendor lock-in**: Presence is entirely within OpenAI's ecosystem. Switching providers would require rebuilding the agent from scratch.

**Pricing opacity**: Without published pricing, budget forecasting is impossible before the sales process. This is a dealbreaker for organizations with strict procurement requirements.

**Limited availability**: As of August 2026, Presence is not broadly available. Rollout is ongoing and prioritized toward large enterprises.

**No self-serve tier**: There's no way to trial Presence without going through the sales process.

## Verdict

OpenAI Presence is a legitimate enterprise offering for organizations that want to offload AI agent deployment entirely. The "we do it for you" model has real value when the alternative is months of internal engineering.

But the pricing opacity, high minimum commitment, and vendor lock-in make it a poor fit for most companies evaluating AI agent tools. If you have the engineering bandwidth, building on Vapi, Retell AI, or directly on the OpenAI Realtime API gives you more control at lower cost.

Contact OpenAI's sales team if Presence looks like a match — just go in prepared to discuss your full call volume, compliance requirements, and integration complexity upfront.
