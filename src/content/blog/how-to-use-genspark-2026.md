Genspark is more than a search engine — it's an agentic research platform that generates structured Sparkpages, runs multi-step research tasks, and can make real phone calls on your behalf. Here's how to get the most out of it from day one.

## Getting Started

**Step 1: Create a free account.** Go to [genspark.ai](https://www.genspark.ai) and sign up. No credit card required. You get 100 credits per day on the free plan, plus AI chat and image generation at zero credits through December 2026.

**Step 2: Understand the credit system.** Credits are consumed by agentic tasks, Sparkpage generation, AI phone calls, and video generation. Standard AI chat and image creation are free through the end of 2026. Check your credit balance in your account dashboard before running heavy tasks.

**Step 3: Choose your task type.** Genspark has two main modes:
- **Chat mode**: Standard conversational AI (free, zero credits)
- **Agent mode**: Multi-step research tasks that produce Sparkpages (credits apply)

## How to Generate a Sparkpage

Sparkpages are Genspark's signature output — structured, cited, shareable research documents.

**1. Enter a research query.** Be specific. Instead of "tell me about CRMs," try: "Compare the top 5 CRM tools for a 10-person B2B sales team, focusing on pipeline management, email integration, and pricing."

**2. Trigger agent mode.** Genspark may automatically switch to agent mode for complex queries, or you can manually select it. Agent mode runs multiple searches and synthesizes findings.

**3. Wait for Sparkpage generation.** More complex queries take longer — typically 30 seconds to 3 minutes. The agent is gathering sources, verifying claims, and structuring the output.

**4. Review and share.** The resulting Sparkpage includes:
- Structured sections with headings
- Cited sources for each claim
- Comparison tables where relevant
- A shareable link you can send to others

**5. Refine with follow-up prompts.** After the Sparkpage generates, you can ask follow-up questions to expand sections, add comparison points, or drill into specific details.

## How to Use the AI Phone Call Feature

Genspark's AI phone call is its most distinctive feature. Here's when and how to use it effectively.

**When to use it:**
- Checking business hours or availability when the website is outdated
- Verifying pricing for local services (restaurants, contractors, stores)
- Gathering information that isn't published online
- Following up on inquiries where a call is more appropriate than email

**How to trigger a phone call:**
1. Enter a prompt like: "Call [business name] at [phone number] and ask about their current pricing for [service]."
2. Genspark will confirm the call details before proceeding.
3. The agent places the call, navigates the conversation, and returns a transcript.

**Tips for better AI phone calls:**
- Provide the exact phone number — Genspark doesn't always look these up accurately
- Be specific about what information you need. Vague calls produce vague transcripts.
- Check the transcript carefully — the AI may have missed nuances or been transferred to hold
- Calls work best for simple, factual information gathering (not negotiation or complex discussions)

## Running Multi-Step Research Agents

For complex tasks that involve multiple sub-questions, Genspark's multi-step agent capability is most valuable.

**Example prompts that work well:**
- "Research the top 5 competitors to [my product], including their pricing, key features, target customers, and recent product updates."
- "Find 10 high-authority backlink opportunities for a [topic] website, including the domain authority, contact method, and a reason why they'd link to us."
- "Analyze the latest research on [topic], summarize the top 5 findings, and identify gaps that need more study."

**How to structure your prompt for best results:**
1. **Define the scope**: What exactly should be covered?
2. **Specify the format**: "Create a table comparing..." or "List each point with a source..."
3. **Set boundaries**: "Focus on 2025–2026 data only" or "Exclude tools over $500/month"
4. **State the audience**: "Written for a non-technical marketing manager"

## Tips for Getting Better Sparkpages

**Use comparison structures.** Genspark excels at "X vs Y" and "top N for Z" formats. These map naturally to Sparkpage sections.

**Iterate after the first Sparkpage.** The first output is a starting point. Follow up with: "Add a section on pricing for each tool" or "Include a recommendation for small teams."

**Ask for citations on specific claims.** If a claim in the Sparkpage looks unsupported, ask: "What's the source for the claim that [specific claim]?"

**Save and share Sparkpages.** Each Sparkpage gets a shareable link. This is useful for sharing research findings with colleagues without requiring them to reproduce the query.

## Managing Your Credits

Credits are the limiting resource on Genspark's free and Plus plans.

**Credit-heavy tasks:**
- Multi-step agent research (5–20+ credits per task)
- AI phone calls (variable, call-length dependent)
- Video generation (most expensive)

**Zero-credit tasks (through Dec 2026):**
- AI chat conversations
- Image generation

**Tips to conserve credits:**
- Use chat mode for exploratory questions before committing credits to a full Sparkpage
- Batch related research into a single comprehensive query rather than multiple small ones
- Check the credit estimate before confirming complex agent tasks

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Vague prompts.** "Research marketing tools" produces a generic Sparkpage. "Compare the top 5 email marketing tools for Shopify stores under 10,000 subscribers, focusing on automation features and pricing" produces a useful one.

**Expecting ChatGPT-style conversation.** Genspark is built for research output, not iterative dialogue. For back-and-forth brainstorming, use [ChatGPT](/blog/chatgpt-review-2026) or [Claude](/blog/claude-review-2026).

**Ignoring credit costs on heavy tasks.** Check your credit balance before running video generation or complex multi-call agent tasks on the free plan.

**Not verifying AI phone call transcripts.** The phone call feature is powerful but not infallible. Always verify critical information (especially pricing or availability) before acting on it.
