Close CRM is a sales-focused CRM built for inside sales teams. Unlike HubSpot or Salesforce, which try to do everything, Close keeps things tight: calling, emailing, SMS, and pipeline management — all in one place without needing dozens of integrations.

But it's not cheap. Here's exactly what you pay for in 2026.

## Close CRM Pricing Plans 2026

| Plan | Price | Users | Included |
|------|-------|-------|----------|
| **Startup** | $49/month | Up to 3 users | Core CRM, calling, emailing, SMS |
| **Professional** | $299/month | Up to 5 users | Everything in Startup + sequences, reporting, bulk actions |
| **Enterprise** | $699/month | Up to 10 users | Everything in Pro + custom roles, API priority, dedicated support |
| **Custom** | Contact sales | 10+ users | Enterprise + volume discounts |

All prices are billed annually. Monthly billing is available at a 10–20% premium.

### Per-User Pricing Note

The plans above are flat-rate for up to the stated number of users. If you need more users:
- Startup: additional users at ~$25/user/month
- Professional: additional users at ~$35/user/month
- Enterprise: custom per-seat pricing

## What's Included in Each Plan

### Startup ($49/month)
The Startup plan is genuinely useful for small teams:
- Unlimited contacts and leads
- Built-in calling (VoIP) with call recording
- Email sync (Gmail, Outlook) + templates
- SMS messaging
- Pipeline management and deal tracking
- Basic reporting
- iOS and Android mobile apps
- API access (standard rate limits)

### Professional ($299/month)
The jump from Startup to Professional is significant both in price and capability:
- Everything in Startup, plus:
- **Sequences**: Multi-step automated follow-up (email, call tasks, SMS)
- **Power Dialer**: Auto-dial through call queues at speed
- **Bulk email**: Send to hundreds of leads at once
- **Advanced reporting**: Leaderboards, activity reports, custom dashboards
- **Multiple pipelines**: Track different sales motions simultaneously
- **Smart Views**: Saved filtered lists with real-time updates
- **Meeting scheduler** integration

### Enterprise ($699/month)
For larger sales operations:
- Everything in Professional, plus:
- **Custom roles and permissions**
- **Higher API rate limits**
- **Priority support** with dedicated account manager
- **Custom onboarding**
- **Advanced security**: SSO, audit logs

## What Makes Close Different from Other CRMs

Close was designed specifically for outbound sales teams. The key differentiators:

**Built-in calling**: Unlike HubSpot or Salesforce where calling is an add-on (often $30–75/user/month extra), Close includes VoIP calling in every plan. Local presence dialing is available on Professional+.

**No "feature gates" on basic functions**: Many CRMs put email templates, bulk actions, or pipeline customization behind expensive tiers. Close's Startup plan is genuinely functional.

**Speed-to-contact focus**: The interface is optimized for high-volume outbound — calling through lead lists, logging calls with one click, automatic call recordings.

## Is Close CRM Worth the Price?

**Yes, if:**
- You have an outbound sales team making 30+ calls/day
- You're tired of paying separately for a dialer + CRM + sequencing tool
- Your team is 1–10 people focused on phone and email outreach
- You value simplicity over customization

**No, if:**
- You need deep marketing automation (use HubSpot)
- You need complex enterprise workflows (use Salesforce)
- You're primarily inbound with low outbound volume
- You need extensive third-party integrations in the CRM itself

## Free Trial

Close offers a **14-day free trial** with full access to Professional features — no credit card required. This is the best way to evaluate whether the calling and sequencing features justify the price for your team.

## Final Thoughts

Close CRM's pricing reflects its positioning: a premium tool for sales teams that live in their dialer. The Startup plan at $49/month for 3 users is competitive when you factor in the built-in calling. The Professional plan at $299/month becomes worthwhile when your team is running sequences and power dialing.

If your sales motion is heavily outbound with calling as the primary channel, Close often replaces 2–3 separate tools at a lower total cost.
