# Vidu AI Pricing 2026: Complete Breakdown

Among fast-growing AI video generators, **Vidu AI pricing** is a common question in 2026 — especially for creators drawn to its reference-to-video feature, which keeps characters and objects consistent across shots. Built by Shengshu Technology, Vidu turns text, images, or reference subjects into short clips and runs on a credit-based model with a free tier. This guide breaks down every plan, how credits become video, and who each tier fits.

## What Is Vidu AI?

Vidu is an AI video generation platform focused on speed and subject consistency. Its standout capability is using reference images to lock a character or product's look across multiple generations. Key features include:

- **Reference-to-video** generation that maintains consistent subjects and styles.
- **Text-to-video and image-to-video** for short clips from a prompt or still.
- **Fast generation** tuned for quick iteration on social content.
- **Template and effect presets** for popular short-form formats.

Because consistency and speed are its edge, Vidu appeals to creators building series content or product videos where the subject must look the same each time.

## Free vs Paid: What Actually Changes

The creative feature set is broadly similar across tiers; what scales is **credit volume, generation quality, speed, and commercial rights**.

**On the Free plan you get:**

- A small monthly credit pool to test reference-to-video and templates.
- Standard queue times and quality.
- Enough to judge whether Vidu's consistency holds up for your content.

**Paid tiers (Standard → Ultimate) unlock:**

- Larger monthly credit pools for more clips.
- Higher-quality output and faster, priority generation.
- On higher tiers, commercial usage rights for client and brand work.

The practical takeaway: choose the tier by output volume. Occasional clips fit Standard; consistent series or product content needs Premium or Ultimate to keep credits from running out.

## Who Each Tier Is For

**Free** — Creators sampling Vidu's consistency and speed before spending.

**Standard (~$8/mo)** — Light creators who post now and then and want more credits than the free tier.

**Premium (~$16–24/mo)** — Regular creators making series or product videos who need reliable quality and speed.

**Ultimate (~$40+/mo)** — High-volume creators and small teams needing big credit pools and commercial rights.

## Tips to Stretch Your Credits

A few habits keep Vidu affordable no matter which tier you land on:

- **Prototype at lower resolution.** Lock your prompt, reference, and motion at standard quality first, then re-render the winner at high resolution — this avoids burning credits on drafts.
- **Reuse reference images.** Vidu's strength is subject consistency, so a single strong reference image can drive an entire series without re-generating the look each time.
- **Batch your ideas.** Because higher tiers include priority queues, grouping generations into one session on Premium or Ultimate gets more finished clips per credit than sporadic one-offs.
