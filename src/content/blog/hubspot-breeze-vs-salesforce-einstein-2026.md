HubSpot Breeze and Salesforce Einstein AI are the two dominant AI layers sitting on top of CRM platforms. Both promise to automate busywork — writing emails, scoring leads, summarizing calls — but they're built for different audiences and come with very different price tags. Here's the full comparison.

## What Are Breeze and Einstein?

**HubSpot Breeze** is HubSpot's AI layer, launched in 2024 and expanded in 2025–2026. It covers content generation, deal intelligence, prospect research, and workflow automation — all embedded inside HubSpot's CRM. Breeze Copilot, Breeze Agents, and Breeze Intelligence are the three main components.

**Salesforce Einstein AI** is Salesforce's long-running AI initiative, now rebuilt on the Agentforce platform (launched 2025). Einstein handles predictive scoring, generative email drafting, call summarization, and fully autonomous sales agents that can take action in CRM without human input.

## Pricing

### HubSpot Breeze

Breeze Copilot is included in all paid HubSpot plans (starting at $20/month for Marketing Hub Starter). More powerful features like Breeze Intelligence require credit purchases:

- **Breeze Intelligence credits**: ~$0.01–0.05 per enrichment action
- **Breeze Agents**: Included in Professional/Enterprise tiers ($800–$3,600+/month)
- **Marketing Hub Professional**: $800/month (where most Breeze Agent features unlock)

See [HubSpot pricing 2026](/blog/hubspot-pricing-2026) for the complete tier breakdown.

### Salesforce Einstein

Einstein is priced as an add-on to Salesforce CRM licenses:

- **Einstein for Sales**: ~$75/user/month (on top of Sales Cloud license)
- **Agentforce**: ~$2/conversation (usage-based, replacing older per-seat model)
- **Data Cloud** (required for enrichment): $108,000+/year for enterprise

Salesforce's total cost is significantly higher. A 50-person sales team using Sales Cloud Professional + Einstein for Sales can exceed $10,000/month before Data Cloud.

## Ease of Use

**HubSpot Breeze** wins on setup speed. Because Breeze is native to HubSpot's UX, sales reps encounter AI features inline — while viewing a contact, writing an email, or reviewing a deal — without context switching. Non-technical admins can configure Breeze Agents using natural language in most cases.

**Salesforce Einstein** is powerful but demands Salesforce admin expertise. Agentforce requires Flow configuration, Apex knowledge for custom actions, and Data Cloud setup for enrichment. The payoff is far greater customization, but the ramp-up is measured in months, not days.

## Which Team Size Is Each Right For?

| Team Size | Recommendation |
|-----------|---------------|
| 1–10 reps | HubSpot Breeze (lower cost, faster setup) |
| 10–50 reps | HubSpot Breeze or Salesforce (budget-dependent) |
| 50–200 reps | Salesforce Einstein if complex pipeline; HubSpot if ops-light |
| 200+ reps | Salesforce Einstein (custom models, Data Cloud, Agentforce ROI scales) |

## Key Scenarios

**Choose HubSpot Breeze if:**
- You're already on HubSpot CRM and don't want a migration
- Your team is non-technical and needs AI out-of-the-box
- Budget is under $3,000/month total for CRM + AI
- You need AI-assisted email writing and basic prospecting without custom model training

**Choose Salesforce Einstein if:**
- You're already on Salesforce and switching CRMs isn't feasible
- You need fully autonomous AI agents that complete multi-step CRM workflows without human approval
- Data Cloud enrichment and Einstein Studio for custom model training are requirements
- Enterprise compliance (Salesforce Shield) is a procurement requirement

## The Bottom Line

HubSpot Breeze is the better value for small to mid-size teams. The AI is good enough for most GTM use cases — email writing, deal intelligence, lead scoring — at a fraction of Salesforce's total cost.

Salesforce Einstein is justified for large enterprises with existing Salesforce infrastructure, complex pipeline logic, and the admin resources to configure Agentforce properly. For those teams, the ROI from autonomous agents at scale outweighs the setup cost.
