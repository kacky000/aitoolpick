Runway restructured their pricing in 2026 and renamed all their plans. The old Unlimited tier is gone; what you're now choosing between is Standard, Pro, and Max — all centered around monthly credit allowances.

Here's the complete breakdown of what each plan costs, what the credits get you, and which tier makes sense for different use cases.

## Runway Gen-4 Pricing Plans (2026)

| Plan | Monthly Billing | Annual Billing | Credits/Month | Watermark |
|------|----------------|----------------|---------------|-----------|
| Free | $0 | $0 | 125 (one-time) | Yes |
| Standard | $15/month | $12/month | 625 | No |
| Pro | $35/month | $28/month | 2,250 | No |
| Max | $95/month | $76/month | 9,500 | No |
| Enterprise | Custom | Custom | Custom | No |

All paid plans include: Gen-4.5, Gen-4, Act-Two (performance capture), Aleph (video editing), Veo 3 and 3.1, all third-party models (Kling 3.0 Pro, Seedance 2.0), FLUX.2 and Seedream 5.0 for images, and watermark removal.

The main difference between plans is not feature access — it's credit volume.

## How Runway Credits Work

Credits are consumed per generation. The exact credit cost varies by model and output length:

| Generation Type | Approx. Credit Cost |
|-----------------|---------------------|
| Gen-4 (5 seconds) | ~50 credits |
| Gen-4 (10 seconds) | ~100 credits |
| Gen-4.5 (5 seconds) | ~55 credits |
| Image generation | ~5–10 credits |
| Act-Two (performance capture) | ~80–120 credits |

**What this means by plan:**
- **Standard (625 credits/mo)**: ~6–12 short video clips per month. Good for light personal use, experimentation, or adding AI elements to a single project.
- **Pro (2,250 credits/mo)**: ~22–45 short clips per month. Viable for regular creative use, small agency projects, or active social content creation.
- **Max (9,500 credits/mo)**: ~95–190 clips per month. For heavy professional use, high-volume social content, or agency work with multiple clients.

Unused credits don't roll over. Credits reset at your billing cycle.

## What Changed in 2026

Runway renamed and restructured their plans:
- Old **Basic** → New **Standard** ($15)
- Old **Standard** → New **Pro** ($35)
- Old **Pro** → New **Max** ($95, with credit allowance raised from 6,750 to 9,500)
- Old **Unlimited** → **Discontinued**

The Unlimited plan removal is the most significant change for heavy users. If you were on Unlimited, you're now on Max with a 9,500 credit cap. For some users, 9,500 credits is more than enough; for high-volume producers, it's a meaningful constraint.

## Which Plan to Choose

**Free plan**: Appropriate if you're evaluating whether AI video generation is useful for your work at all. The 125 one-time credits are enough for ~2–3 short tests. The watermark makes it unusable for client work.

**Standard ($15/month)**: Right for individuals who want occasional AI video clips for personal projects, YouTube experiments, or social media without heavy volume. 625 credits/month is roughly 6–12 clips per month — sustainable for light use.

**Pro ($35/month)**: The sweet spot for most active users. 2,250 credits supports consistent output — a social media manager running AI content for 1–2 brands, a small creative studio doing occasional client work, or an independent filmmaker experimenting with AI elements. This is where most professional-but-not-high-volume users land.

**Max ($95/month)**: For agencies, high-volume content creators, or anyone doing 100+ short AI video clips per month. At this volume, the per-credit cost drops and the plan pays for itself if you're billing client work or monetizing the content.

**Enterprise**: Covers teams with custom needs — higher volume, team-level sharing, priority generation queues, and SLA guarantees. Contact sales for pricing.

## Credit Overage and Add-Ons

When you run out of credits, you can purchase additional credits in bundles rather than upgrading plans. Pricing for credit add-ons isn't published transparently — it's available in the Runway dashboard when you're logged in. Expect to pay a premium over the per-credit cost implied by monthly plans.

If you're consistently buying credit top-ups, upgrading to the next plan tier is almost always more cost-effective.

## The Free Tier Limitation

The 125 free credits are a one-time allocation, not monthly. Once they're spent, you're on paid plans. There's no free monthly credit renewal. This is a notable difference from some competitors (like Pika) that offer small monthly free allocations.

## Is Runway Worth the Cost?

Runway is competitive with alternatives when you consider that a single subscription covers multiple state-of-the-art video and image models. The Gen-4.5 quality for realistic human motion is among the best available. Act-Two (performance capture from reference video) is a unique feature not widely available elsewhere.

For teams doing serious video production work or agencies billing AI video work to clients, the cost is justifiable. For casual users who want occasional AI clips, Standard at $15 is a reasonable experiment. The elimination of Unlimited is the main pain point for power users who need uncapped generation.

The annual discount (20–22% savings) is worth taking if you're committing to the tool beyond 3 months.
