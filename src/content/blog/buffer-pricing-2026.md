Buffer is one of the few social media tools that still offers a genuinely useful free plan in 2026. If you're evaluating it, the pricing structure is simple but has one key wrinkle that trips people up: you pay per channel, not per user.

Here's exactly what you get at each tier and how to figure out which plan makes sense for your situation.

## Buffer Pricing Plans in 2026

Buffer has three tiers: Free, Essentials, and Team. All paid plans are priced per channel per month.

| Plan | Price | Channels | Users | Posts per Channel |
|------|-------|----------|-------|-------------------|
| Free | $0 | 3 | 1 | 10 posts/channel |
| Essentials | $6/channel/month (annual) | Unlimited | 1 | Unlimited |
| Team | $12/channel/month (annual) | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |

*Annual billing. Monthly billing is available at ~25% higher rates.*

### The Per-Channel Pricing Model

This is Buffer's biggest differentiator — and what makes it dramatically cheaper than Hootsuite for smaller operations.

Instead of paying per user seat (like Hootsuite's $99-$399/seat model), Buffer charges per social channel. That means a solo creator managing 5 channels on the Essentials plan pays $30/month. A team of 10 sharing those same 5 channels on Team plan pays $60/month — total.

Compare that to Hootsuite's Professional plan at $199/seat, where a 3-person team pays $597/month.

For small teams, Buffer's model almost always works out cheaper. The exception is agencies managing dozens of client accounts — at that scale, per-channel pricing adds up.

### Free Plan

Buffer's free plan is more useful than most "freemium" social tools. You get:

- 3 channels (connect any combination of Instagram, Facebook, X, LinkedIn, TikTok, Pinterest, YouTube, Mastodon)
- 10 scheduled posts per channel at any time
- AI Assistant for caption writing and hashtag suggestions
- Basic analytics (engagement, reach, impressions)
- Link in Bio page

The 10-post-per-channel limit is a queue limit, not a monthly cap. Once a post publishes and frees up a slot, you can schedule another. In practice, for light posting schedules (1-2 posts per day), the free plan works fine.

**What's missing on Free**: You can't add team members, can't access extended analytics or historical data beyond 30 days, and there's no approval workflow. The analytics are genuinely basic — useful for monitoring, not for client reporting.

### Essentials Plan ($6/channel/month)

Essentials removes the 10-post limit and adds extended analytics with 90 days of historical data. For solo creators or individual marketers managing their own accounts, this is usually the right tier.

At $6/channel annually:
- 3 channels: $18/month
- 5 channels: $30/month
- 10 channels: $60/month

The analytics improvement is meaningful. You get engagement metrics, audience demographics, follower growth trends, and post performance breakdowns. Not the deepest analytics in the market, but sufficient for most content creators and small businesses.

**Still missing on Essentials**: You're limited to 1 user. No team collaboration, no approval workflows. This is a solo tier.

### Team Plan ($12/channel/month)

Team doubles the per-channel price but unlocks unlimited users, approval workflows, and team collaboration features. This is where Buffer becomes viable for actual teams.

At $12/channel annually:
- 3 channels: $36/month
- 5 channels: $60/month
- 10 channels: $120/month

A team of 5 managing 5 channels pays $60/month total. At Hootsuite Professional, that same team pays $995/month. The math is stark.

**Approval workflows**: A team member drafts a post, a manager reviews and approves before it goes live. This is the key feature that separates Team from Essentials.

**Unlimited users**: You can add clients, stakeholders, or as many team members as needed without incremental cost. This is unusually generous compared to competitors.

### Nonprofit Discount

Buffer offers a 50% discount to registered nonprofits. If you're working with a nonprofit, contact their support team with documentation — it brings Team down to $6/channel/month, which is exceptional value.

## What Buffer Does Well

**Simplicity**: Buffer's interface is genuinely clean. Scheduling a post takes fewer clicks than most competitors. If you've tried tools like Hootsuite and found the UI overwhelming, Buffer is a deliberate contrast.

**The Start Page (Link in Bio)**: Buffer includes a customizable link-in-bio landing page on all plans including Free. It's a nice included feature that competes directly with tools like Linktree.

**Consistent publishing**: Buffer's reliability for actually sending scheduled posts is high. There are fewer horror stories about posts not going out compared to some competitors.

**Ideas feature**: Buffer has a built-in ideas section where you can jot down content concepts before turning them into scheduled posts. Small thing, but useful for solo creators.

## Where Buffer Falls Short

**Analytics ceiling**: Even on Team, Buffer's analytics are adequate but not impressive. If you need detailed competitor benchmarking, share-of-voice data, or presentation-ready executive reports, you'll hit the ceiling.

**Social listening**: Buffer has no social listening capability. You can't monitor mentions, keywords, or brand conversations across social platforms.

**No TikTok auto-publish on all plans**: Depending on your account type and region, TikTok publishing may require manual confirmation. This is a TikTok API limitation, not just Buffer, but worth knowing.

**Limited integrations**: Buffer integrates with fewer third-party tools than Hootsuite. If your workflow depends on connecting social tools to CRMs, project management platforms, or data warehouses, this may be limiting.

## Who Buffer Is Right For

Buffer is the right tool if:
- You're a creator, freelancer, or small business managing your own social presence
- You want an honest free plan to start with before committing money
- Your team is small and budget-conscious
- You value simplicity over feature depth
- You're not doing serious competitive analytics or client reporting

It's probably not the right tool if:
- You need deep social listening and competitive intelligence
- You're running an agency with 20+ client accounts where per-channel pricing stacks up
- Your clients expect polished, presentation-ready analytics reports
- You need CRM integration or advanced workflow automation

## The Bottom Line

Buffer's pricing model is honest and transparent. The per-channel structure means small teams genuinely pay less than they would with seat-based competitors — sometimes dramatically less.

The free plan is a real starting point, not a locked-down demo. The jump from Free to Essentials ($6/channel) is reasonable if you're posting consistently and want reliable analytics. Team at $12/channel makes sense once you need approval workflows or multiple collaborators.

If you're evaluating Buffer, start with the free plan. If you consistently run up against the 10-post queue limit or need analytics beyond 30 days, the upgrade to Essentials is easy to justify.
