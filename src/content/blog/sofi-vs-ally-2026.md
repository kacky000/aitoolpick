**SoFi vs Ally Bank** is one of the most common comparisons for people moving away from traditional banks. Both are FDIC-insured online banks with no monthly fees, competitive APYs, and solid mobile apps — but they serve slightly different types of customers.

## Quick Verdict

- **Choose SoFi** if you want a bundled financial platform covering banking, investing, loans, and credit, all in one app.
- **Choose Ally** if you want a straightforward, no-fuss online bank with consistently strong savings rates and excellent customer service.

## Pricing Compared

*APY rates are variable and may change. Always verify current rates directly with each bank before opening an account.*

SoFi's higher savings APY requires you to set up direct deposit; without it, the rate drops noticeably. Ally's rate applies without conditions, which may suit people who don't want to meet requirements.

## Savings Account

**SoFi's High-Yield Savings** is part of its combined Checking + Savings account. To unlock the top APY, you need to receive a direct deposit each month. If you do, the rate is competitive. SoFi also offers savings "Vaults," letting you set aside money for specific goals within the same account.

**Ally's Online Savings** requires no direct deposit to earn its headline rate. Ally also offers "Buckets" for goal-based saving and a round-up feature to automate small transfers. For people who want a dedicated savings account without strings attached, Ally may feel more straightforward.

## Checking Account

**SoFi Checking** earns 0.50% APY — unusually high for a checking account — and comes with early paycheck access (up to two days early with direct deposit). The debit card works at 60,000+ Allpoint ATMs fee-free.

**Ally Spending Account** earns 0.10% APY, which is modest but still more than most traditional bank checking accounts. Ally reimburses up to $10 per month in out-of-network ATM fees, adding flexibility if you use ATMs outside the Allpoint network.

## Loans and Investing

**SoFi** is built around the idea of being a one-stop financial shop. Beyond banking, it offers student loan refinancing, personal loans, home loans, and brokerage accounts (including fractional shares and crypto). If you want to consolidate your financial life into one app, SoFi's breadth is hard to match.

**Ally** also offers home loans, auto loans, and investment accounts through Ally Invest — but the investing platform is more basic than SoFi's. Ally's real edge is in banking products: its CD lineup, with terms ranging from 3 months to 5 years, could be appealing if you want to lock in a rate for a specific period.

## Customer Service

Both banks are online-only, so in-person help isn't available with either. **Ally** has a long-standing reputation for excellent customer service — 24/7 phone support, low hold times, and consistently high satisfaction scores. **SoFi** offers 24/7 chat and phone support as well, but customer service reviews are more mixed.

## Who Should Pick Which?

- **All-in-one financial platform** → SoFi. Banking, investing, loans, and credit cards under one roof.
- **Best savings rate without conditions** → Ally may suit more, since no direct deposit is required.
- **High checking APY** → SoFi's 0.50% is a genuine differentiator.
- **CDs and fixed-term savings** → Ally's extensive CD ladder options could be the better fit.
- **Established customer service reputation** → Ally has the edge here.

## Verdict

If you want a **complete financial platform** and plan to use banking, investing, and loans in one place, SoFi is worth exploring. If you want a **reliable, no-conditions online savings account** with excellent support and a variety of CD options, Ally may be the better long-term home for your deposits.

That said, many people use both: SoFi for lending and investing, Ally for their core savings. There's no rule saying you have to pick just one.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is SoFi or Ally better for savings?

Both offer competitive high-yield savings rates. SoFi's top APY may be slightly higher, but it requires a monthly direct deposit. Ally's rate applies without conditions, which could be simpler for some savers.

### Does SoFi or Ally have physical branches?

Neither. Both are online-only banks. If in-person banking is important, a traditional bank or credit union may be worth considering instead.

### Which is safer — SoFi or Ally?

Both are FDIC-insured up to $250,000 per depositor. Neither is "safer" than the other from a deposit-protection standpoint.

### Can I use SoFi and Ally at the same time?

Yes. Many people keep a high-yield savings account at Ally while using SoFi for loans or investing. There's no requirement to use only one.
