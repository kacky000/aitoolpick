# Google Antigravity Pricing 2026: Free, Pro, Ultra — Complete Breakdown

Google Antigravity's pricing works differently from most AI coding tools. Instead of a standalone subscription, capacity is bundled with **Google AI plans** — the same plans that cover Gemini access, Google One storage, and other Google services. Here's exactly what you get at each tier in October 2026.

## Antigravity Plan Overview

| Plan | Monthly Price | Annual Option | AI Capacity |
|------|--------------|---------------|-------------|
| Individual (Free) | $0 | — | Capped daily requests |
| Pro | $19.99/month | ~$16.67/month | Standard |
| Ultra 5x | $99.99/month | ~$83/month | 5× vs Pro |
| Ultra 20x | $199.99/month | ~$167/month | 20× vs Pro |
| Pay-as-you-go | $25 per 2,500 credits | — | Overage currency |

Google updated this structure at Google I/O in May 2026, reducing the top tier from $249.99 to $199.99 and adding the $99.99 Ultra 5x tier.

## Free Individual Plan

The free tier gives you access to Gemini 3 Pro via Antigravity with a daily request cap. Practically, you can run 15–25 moderate coding tasks per day before hitting limits — enough for casual use or evaluation.

What you get free:
- Full access to the Antigravity desktop app and CLI
- Gemini 3 Pro completions and chat (capped)
- Basic subagent workflows (1 agent at a time)
- 2 background scheduled tasks

The free tier is more capable than GitHub Copilot's free plan but less generous than Claude Code's (which has no free tier at all — you need an Anthropic subscription).

## Pro Plan — $19.99/month

Pro is the standard tier for individual professional developers. At $19.99/month, it undercuts Claude Code's entry price and is roughly equal to Perplexity Pro.

Pro includes:
- Uncapped Gemini 3 Pro access (subject to fair use)
- Multi-model access: Claude Sonnet 4.6, GPT-OSS-120B
- Parallel multi-agent execution (up to 3 concurrent agents)
- Unlimited background task scheduling
- Voice command interface
- 500 AI credits/month for overage

Pro is the right tier for most developers using Antigravity as their primary AI coding tool.

## Ultra 5x — $99.99/month

Ultra 5x is aimed at power users and developers working on large, long-running projects. The 5× capacity multiplier means you can run substantially more parallel agents and longer autonomous sessions before hitting soft limits.

Additional Ultra 5x features:
- Up to 8 concurrent parallel agents
- Priority model routing (lower latency at peak times)
- 3,000 AI credits/month
- Early access to new Antigravity features

## Ultra 20x — $199.99/month

Ultra 20x is for developers spending significant work hours inside Antigravity with continuous agent activity — think teams running overnight refactors, large migration projects, or high-frequency deployment automation.

- Up to 20 concurrent parallel agents
- Maximum priority routing
- 15,000 AI credits/month
- Dedicated support channel

## Pay-as-You-Go Credits

Credits ($25 per 2,500) are an overage currency available on any paid plan. If you hit your monthly capacity, requests draw from your credit balance rather than failing. This prevents hard cutoffs on critical work.

For most Pro users, the 500 included monthly credits cover overflow without needing to purchase extra.

Antigravity's $19.99 entry is competitive with Cursor and Claude Code. The free tier makes it the lowest-risk option to evaluate.

## Is Antigravity Worth the Price?

**Free tier**: Yes, unambiguously — try it before spending anything.

**Pro ($19.99)**: Worth it if you're working in GCP environments, want multi-model flexibility, or need parallel subagents for complex features. At roughly the same price as Cursor and Claude Code, the decision comes down to workflow fit.

**Ultra ($99.99+)**: Only justified for developers running Antigravity as their primary development automation platform with heavy continuous usage.
