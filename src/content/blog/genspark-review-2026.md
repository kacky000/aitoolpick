Genspark is an agentic AI search engine that goes beyond retrieving links — it executes multi-step research tasks, generates structured Sparkpages from its findings, and can even make AI phone calls on your behalf. Launched in 2024 and updated throughout 2026, it sits in a growing category between traditional AI chatbots and autonomous agent platforms. Here's what it actually delivers.

## What Is Genspark?

Genspark combines a search interface with an agent layer. When you enter a query, it doesn't just return results — it dispatches sub-agents to gather information from multiple sources, synthesize findings, and produce a Sparkpage: a structured, shareable document with sections, citations, and visual formatting.

This makes it fundamentally different from [Perplexity](/blog/perplexity-review-2026) (which focuses on sourced web answers) and [ChatGPT](/blog/chatgpt-review-2026) (which generates responses from its training data). Genspark's output is more like a mini-research report than a chat response.

## Pricing

Genspark runs on a credit-based model. See [Genspark pricing 2026](/blog/genspark-pricing-2026) for the full breakdown. Quick summary:

- **Free**: 100 credits/day, no credit card required
- **Plus**: $24.99/month (or ~$20/month annual)
- **Pro**: $249.99/month (heavy agentic workloads)
- **Team**: $30/seat/month

Note: AI chat and image generation cost **zero credits** through December 31, 2026 — a significant promotional offer that makes the free tier genuinely useful right now.

## What Genspark Does Well

**Sparkpage quality.** For research-heavy queries ("compare the top 5 CRMs for a 20-person sales team"), Genspark produces a structured, citation-backed document that would take hours to assemble manually. The output is formatted, scannable, and shareable — more like a deliverable than a chat response.

**AI phone calls.** This is Genspark's most distinctive feature. You can ask it to call a restaurant to check reservation availability, call a business to verify hours, or gather pricing information. The agent handles the call and returns a transcript. No other mainstream AI tool offers this out of the box.

**Free tier for real work.** 100 credits per day is meaningful if you're doing standard research tasks. Combined with free AI chat and image generation (through 2026), the free plan competes with paid tiers of some competitors.

**Multi-step task chaining.** Complex requests — "research the top competitors to my SaaS product, find their pricing, and summarize their strengths and weaknesses" — get decomposed into subtasks and executed in sequence. The result is closer to what you'd expect from a junior analyst than a search engine.

## Where Genspark Falls Short

**Credit burn on heavy tasks.** Video generation, AI phone calls, and complex research agents eat credits fast. The free tier's 100 credits/day disappears quickly if you're running multi-step agent tasks repeatedly.

**Pro plan pricing is steep.** At $249.99/month, the Pro plan is priced for power users with serious research or automation workloads. Most individual users will find the Plus plan sufficient.

**Newer product, evolving quality.** Genspark's agent reliability varies by task type. Well-defined research queries produce excellent results; open-ended or ambiguous requests sometimes return unfocused Sparkpages that need significant manual cleanup.

**Less conversational than ChatGPT.** Genspark isn't optimized for back-and-forth chat. For iterative conversations, brainstorming, or coding help, ChatGPT or Claude are better fits.

## Who Should Use Genspark

**Good fit:**
- Researchers and analysts who regularly produce structured reports
- Marketers doing competitive intelligence and market research
- Business owners who need to gather real-world info (via AI phone calls)
- Power users who want more than a chat interface from their AI tool

**Not a fit:**
- Users who primarily want a conversational AI chatbot
- Developers looking for API access to build on top of (limited compared to OpenAI/Anthropic)
- Teams needing deep enterprise security and compliance features

## The Verdict

Genspark is genuinely useful for research-heavy workflows where you need structured output, not just a chat response. The AI phone call feature is a compelling differentiator. The free tier is strong enough to evaluate it without financial commitment.

The core weakness is the credit model — heavy agentic tasks drain your allowance quickly, and the pricing jump from Plus to Pro is significant. For most users, the Plus plan at $24.99/month is the right entry point if the free tier leaves you wanting more.
