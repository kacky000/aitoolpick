# InVideo AI Pricing 2026: Free, Plus, and Max Plans Explained

InVideo AI generates full videos from a text prompt — script, voiceover, stock footage, and edits included. In 2026 its pricing is metered by generation minutes per week. Here's the complete breakdown of what each plan costs and includes.

## Pricing Plans at a Glance

| Plan | Monthly Cost | Generation Minutes | Watermark |
|------|-------------|-------------------|-----------|
| Free | $0 | ~10 min/week | Yes |
| Plus | ~$20 ($16 annual) | ~50 min/month | No |
| Max | ~$48 ($40 annual) | ~200 min/month | No |
| Enterprise | Custom | Custom + team | No |

## How "Generation Minutes" Work

InVideo AI bills by the minutes of video you generate, not the number of prompts or edits. Each time you create or regenerate a video, its runtime is deducted from your allowance. Iterating heavily on one video can quietly consume your quota, so plan for revisions.

## Free Plan ($0)

The free tier gives you a small weekly generation allowance with a watermark. It's enough to see how the AI turns a prompt into a scripted, voiced, and edited video — but not enough for regular publishing.

**Best for:** Testing the text-to-video workflow before committing.

## Plus Plan (~$20/month, or ~$16 billed annually)

Plus removes the watermark, unlocks a solid monthly minute pool, adds premium stock media, voice cloning, and higher export quality. For a solo creator or marketer producing several videos a month, this is the practical starting tier.

**Best for:** Individual creators and small businesses publishing regularly.

## Max Plan (~$48/month, or ~$40 billed annually)

Max roughly quadruples generation minutes and raises limits across the board — more voice clones, more premium media, and priority generation. It suits agencies and creators producing high volumes.

**Best for:** Power users, agencies, and content teams shipping many videos monthly.

## Enterprise Plan (Custom)

Enterprise adds team seats, collaboration, custom minute pools, and dedicated support.

## How InVideo AI Compares on Price

| Tool | Entry Paid Price | Approach |
|------|-----------------|----------|
| InVideo AI Plus | ~$20/mo | Text-to-full-video generation |
| Opus Clip Starter | ~$9/mo | Repurpose long video into clips |
| Runway | ~$15/mo | Generative AI clips + effects |
| Descript | ~$16/mo | Edit video like a document |

InVideo AI's differentiator is end-to-end generation from a prompt — it writes and assembles the whole video, where tools like Descript assume you already have footage.

## Is InVideo AI Worth It?

### Paid pays off when:
- You need finished videos fast without filming or editing
- The free watermark and weekly cap block real publishing
- Voiceover and stock media would otherwise cost extra

### Free is enough when:
- You're only experimenting with text-to-video
- You produce videos rarely

## Tips to Optimize InVideo AI Costs

1. **Nail the prompt first** — fewer regenerations means fewer minutes burned
2. **Choose annual billing** — the discount is significant on both tiers
3. **Right-size your plan** — start on Plus, upgrade to Max only if you cap out
4. **Reuse scripts** — refine text before regenerating the full video

## Bottom Line

InVideo AI is priced for creators who want finished videos from a prompt, not raw footage to edit. Plus at ~$16/month annually covers a typical solo output; Max is for high-volume teams. Watch your generation minutes — revisions are where the quota goes.
