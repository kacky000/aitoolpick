# Sudowrite Pricing 2026: Complete Breakdown

If you write fiction and you have been shopping for an AI co-writer, Sudowrite pricing is probably one of the first things you want to understand. Unlike marketing-focused tools, Sudowrite is built specifically for novelists, and its plans are measured in monthly AI credits (or "words") rather than seats or generic templates. This guide breaks down every 2026 tier, what you actually get, and which plan matches your writing pace so you do not overpay or run dry mid-chapter.

## What Sudowrite Is

Sudowrite is an AI writing app designed from the ground up for storytellers, not blog marketers. It focuses on the messy, creative work of drafting and revising long-form fiction. Its core features include:

- **Story Bible** — a structured place to store your premise, characters, worldbuilding, outline, and synopsis so the AI stays consistent across a whole novel.
- **Write** — continues your prose in your voice, generating the next few paragraphs when you hit a wall.
- **Describe** — expands a moment using sensory detail (sight, sound, smell, metaphor) so your scenes feel vivid.
- **Rewrite** — reworks a passage to be more descriptive, more concise, more intense, or in a different tone.

Additional tools like Brainstorm, Expand, and Canvas help with idea generation and plotting. The through-line is that every feature serves narrative writing, which is why serious novelists gravitate to it over general-purpose assistants.

## Tiers Explained

Sudowrite uses a credit-based model. Each plan grants a monthly pool of AI words (credits) that you spend as you generate, rewrite, and describe. Heavier or more advanced models consume credits faster, so your effective word count depends on how you write.

- **Hobby & Student** — the entry tier for casual writers, students, and anyone testing the waters. Enough credits for steady progress on a single project, but you will notice the ceiling if you draft daily.
- **Professional** — the sweet spot for working novelists. A large monthly credit pool plus access to premium models, making it realistic to draft a full manuscript across a couple of months.
- **Max** — for prolific or full-time authors juggling multiple books, deadlines, or heavy revision cycles. The largest credit allotment and priority access to the newest models.

## Who Each Tier Suits

**Hobby & Student (~$10/mo).** Choose this if you write occasionally, are finishing your first short novel, or simply want to see whether an AI co-writer fits your process. The credit pool covers meaningful drafting, but if you rely on Write and Rewrite constantly, you may exhaust it before month's end.

**Professional (~$22/mo).** This is the plan most committed novelists land on. The roughly one-million-word monthly pool means you can draft, describe, and rewrite freely through a normal writing schedule, and premium model access noticeably improves prose quality and consistency. If you are seriously trying to finish a book, start here.

**Max (~$44/mo).** Pick Max if writing is your job. Authors publishing several titles a year, running heavy revision passes, or generating a lot of exploratory material will appreciate the doubled credit ceiling and priority access to newer models. For everyone else, it is more capacity than a single project needs.

## Comparison vs General AI Writers

General AI writing platforms are optimized for marketing copy, SEO articles, and short-form business content. Their templates, tone controls, and team features assume you are producing ads or blog posts, not a 90,000-word novel. Sudowrite flips that: the Story Bible keeps character and plot details coherent across an entire manuscript, and features like Describe and Rewrite are tuned for prose craft rather than conversion rate.

Price-wise, Sudowrite sits in a similar band to mainstream assistants but sells capacity by creative output. Sudowrite pricing is straightforward once you think in monthly credits instead of seats. Because it is purpose-built for fiction, it delivers value that generic tools cannot match for novelists, even if the sticker price looks comparable.
