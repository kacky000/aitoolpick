# Otter.ai Pricing 2026: Free, Pro, Business, and Enterprise Plans

Otter.ai is one of the best-known AI meeting assistants, transcribing calls in real time and generating summaries and action items. In 2026 it runs a tiered plan structure built around monthly transcription minutes. Here's exactly what each plan costs and includes.

## Pricing Plans at a Glance

| Plan | Monthly Cost (Annual) | Transcription | Best For |
|------|----------------------|---------------|----------|
| Basic | $0 | ~300 min/mo, 30 min/convo | Light users |
| Pro | ~$10 | ~1,200 min/mo | Individuals |
| Business | ~$20 | ~6,000 min/mo | Teams |
| Enterprise | Custom | Advanced admin/security | Organizations |

## How Otter's Pricing Works

Otter meters usage primarily in monthly transcription minutes plus per-conversation length limits. Higher tiers raise those caps and add features like team collaboration, admin controls, and advanced security. Annual billing lowers the monthly rate versus paying month to month.

## Basic Plan ($0)

The free tier includes real-time transcription, AI meeting summaries, and a monthly minute allowance with a per-conversation cap. It's enough for occasional meetings and testing the assistant.

**Best for:** Light users and evaluation.

## Pro Plan (~$10/month billed annually)

Pro raises the monthly minute pool and per-conversation limit, adds advanced search, exports, and more custom vocabulary. For an individual professional in regular meetings, it's the practical tier.

**Best for:** Individuals with frequent calls.

## Business Plan (~$20/month billed annually)

Business multiplies minutes, adds team features, admin controls, usage analytics, and shared workflows. It's aimed at teams standardizing on Otter for meeting notes.

**Best for:** Teams and departments.

## Enterprise (Custom)

Enterprise adds advanced security, SSO, and organization-wide administration under custom pricing.

**Best for:** Large organizations with compliance needs.

Otter's differentiator is polished live transcription and a long track record, though free-first rivals now pressure its entry pricing.

## Is Otter Worth Paying For?

### Paid pays off when:
- You exceed the free minute cap regularly
- You need longer conversations and better search
- Your team wants shared notes and admin controls

### Free is enough when:
- You have occasional, short meetings
- Basic summaries meet your needs

## Tips to Optimize Otter Costs

1. **Track your monthly minutes** to pick the right tier
2. **Go annual** for the lower effective price
3. **Compare against free-first tools** if budget is tight
4. **Use custom vocabulary** to cut editing time

## Bottom Line

Otter.ai's pricing spans a usable free tier, ~$10 Pro, ~$20 Business, and custom Enterprise — all metered by transcription minutes. It's a strong, established choice, but check your minute needs and weigh free-first rivals like Fathom before committing.
