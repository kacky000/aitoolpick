# Frase Pricing 2026: Solo, Basic, and Team Plans Explained

Frase is an AI SEO content tool that researches SERPs, builds content briefs, and writes optimized drafts. In 2026 its pricing has a twist: the plans cover the writing tool, but the SEO optimization score is a separate add-on. Here's the full breakdown so there are no surprises.

## Pricing Plans at a Glance

| Plan | Monthly Cost | Documents | Users |
|------|-------------|-----------|-------|
| Free Trial | $0 (limited) | A few documents | 1 |
| Basic | ~$15 | ~30 documents/mo | 1 |
| Team | ~$115 | Unlimited documents | 3+ |
| Enterprise | Custom | Unlimited + admin | Custom |
| SEO Add-on | ~$35 | Adds SEO optimization | Any plan |

## How Documents Work

A "document" in Frase is one piece of content you research, brief, and write. Basic caps you at roughly 30 documents per month; Team removes the cap. If you publish a handful of articles monthly, Basic is fine; content agencies need Team.

## The SEO Add-on Catch

Frase's headline optimization feature — the SEO score that grades your draft against top-ranking pages — is not included in Basic or Team by default. It's a ~$35/month add-on. Factor this in: for a solo user, "Frase for SEO" realistically costs ~$50/month (Basic + Add-on), not $15.

## Free Trial

Frase offers a low-cost or time-limited trial so you can test SERP research and brief building before committing. It's enough to judge the workflow.

**Best for:** Evaluating whether Frase fits your content process.

## Basic Plan (~$15/month)

Basic includes SERP research, AI writing, content briefs, and outlines for one user with a monthly document cap. Without the add-on, you get the research and drafting engine but not the optimization score.

**Best for:** Solo bloggers and freelancers publishing a few articles a month.

## Team Plan (~$115/month)

Team unlocks unlimited documents, multiple seats, and collaboration features. For agencies and in-house content teams producing many articles, the per-document math quickly favors Team.

**Best for:** Content teams and agencies at scale.

## How Frase Compares on Price

| Tool | Entry Price | Focus |
|------|------------|-------|
| Frase Basic | ~$15/mo (+$35 SEO) | SERP research + briefs + writing |
| Surfer SEO | ~$79/mo | Deep on-page optimization |
| Writesonic | ~$16/mo | AI article writing + SEO |
| Semrush | ~$139/mo | Full SEO suite |

Frase sits between a pure writer and a full SEO suite: strong at research and briefs, with optimization available as an add-on.

## Is Frase Worth It?

### It's worth it when:
- You want SERP-driven briefs that shortcut research
- You publish enough to benefit from templated workflows
- You'll pay for the add-on to get the optimization score

### Look elsewhere when:
- You want optimization included, not bolted on (Surfer, Writesonic)
- You need a full SEO platform (Semrush)

## Tips to Optimize Frase Costs

1. **Budget for the add-on** — "Frase for SEO" is really ~$50/mo solo
2. **Match plan to volume** — stay on Basic until you exceed ~30 docs/month
3. **Use it for briefs even without the add-on** — research alone saves hours
4. **Consolidate seats on Team** — unlimited documents pays off with volume

## Bottom Line

Frase's real cost depends on whether you need the SEO Add-on — most SEO users do, pushing the effective price to ~$50/month solo. For research and brief-building, it's excellent value; just don't mistake the $15 sticker price for the full SEO experience.
