Two hundred dollars a month is real money. Claude Max and ChatGPT Pro sit at the top of the AI chatbot pricing ladder, and both promise dramatically more capability than their $20/month siblings. But what do you actually get — and does the math work out?

## The Plans at a Glance

**Claude Max** comes in two tiers:
- **Max 5x** ($100/month): 5× more usage than Claude Pro ($20/month)
- **Max 20x** ($200/month): 20× more usage than Claude Pro

Both tiers use Claude Sonnet 4.6 and Opus 4.6, with the Max 20x unlocking higher limits on Projects, extended thinking, and document analysis.

**ChatGPT Pro** also has two tiers:
- **Pro $100/month**: 5× the usage of Plus, access to GPT-5.6 Sol, Codex, Deep Research
- **Pro $200/month**: 20× usage, adds **unlimited Sora video generation** and the Operator agent

## What You're Actually Paying For

### Claude Max ($100 or $200/month)

The core value proposition is **context and depth**. Claude's 200K token context window means you can feed it entire codebases, long research documents, or lengthy contracts and have coherent conversations across all of it. The Max tiers simply remove the rate limits that Pro users hit mid-day.

Claude Max is the choice for:
- Lawyers and consultants analyzing long documents
- Developers working on large codebases with Projects
- Researchers synthesizing multi-source literature
- Power users who routinely hit Pro's daily limits

If you've never been rate-limited on Claude Pro, you don't need Max.

### ChatGPT Pro ($100 or $200/month)

ChatGPT Pro's differentiators are **tool breadth and multimodality**. You get:
- Deeper access to Deep Research (multi-hour autonomous research runs)
- Codex for autonomous coding tasks
- The Operator agent for web-based task automation
- At $200/month: **unlimited Sora video generation** — the biggest feature gap between the two tiers

ChatGPT Pro is the choice for:
- Marketers and content creators who use Sora for video
- Business analysts who rely on Deep Research for market intelligence
- Developers automating multi-step web tasks with Operator
- Power users who need everything in one subscription

## The $100 vs $200 Question

For Claude, the difference between Max 5x ($100) and Max 20x ($200) is almost entirely usage volume. If you're a very heavy user hitting limits on Max 5x, upgrade. Otherwise, start at $100.

For ChatGPT, the jump from $100 to $200 is about **Sora**. If you don't need AI video generation, $100/month covers everything else. Unlimited Sora is primarily valuable for content creators, ad agencies, and social media managers producing high volumes of short-form video.

## Practical Recommendation

**Get Claude Max if:**
- Your work involves analyzing or synthesizing long documents
- You primarily need writing, coding, or research assistance
- You consistently hit rate limits on Claude Pro

**Get ChatGPT Pro if:**
- You need AI video generation (Sora)
- You rely on Deep Research for competitive intelligence
- You want one subscription for the broadest AI tool coverage

**Skip both if:**
- You're rarely rate-limited on Claude Pro or ChatGPT Plus
- You can split the cost across multiple $20/month tools

At $200/month, the bar is high. Run your own usage numbers from the past month before committing.
