# Hailuo AI Pricing 2026: Free Credits, Plans, and Value Explained

Hailuo AI, from MiniMax, became one of the most talked-about text-to-video generators for its sharp motion and realistic output — often at a lower price than Western rivals. In 2026 it runs a free-credits-plus-subscription model. Here's the full breakdown.

## Pricing Plans at a Glance

| Plan | Monthly Cost (Annual) | Credits | Best For |
|------|----------------------|---------|----------|
| Free | $0 | Daily/monthly free credits | Testing |
| Standard | ~$10 | Larger pool, no watermark | Creators |
| Pro/Unlimited | ~$25+ | High volume, priority, HD | Professionals |

## How Hailuo's Credits Work

Hailuo grants credits that are spent per video generation, with cost varying by length, resolution, and model version. The free plan refreshes a limited credit allowance; paid tiers add larger pools, remove watermarks, unlock higher resolution, and speed up generation. Annual billing lowers the monthly rate.

## Free Plan ($0)

Hailuo's free tier is unusually generous, refreshing credits that let you generate multiple short clips regularly. Output may be watermarked or limited in resolution, but it's enough to seriously evaluate quality.

**Best for:** Testing and light, casual video creation.

## Standard Plan (~$10/month billed annually)

Standard expands your credit pool, removes watermarks, and unlocks higher quality. For creators making regular short-form video, it's an affordable working tier versus pricier competitors.

**Best for:** Content creators on a budget.

## Pro / Unlimited (~$25+/month)

Higher tiers offer large or unlimited generation, priority queues, and top resolution — for professionals producing video at scale.

**Best for:** Studios and high-volume creators.

## Is Hailuo Worth Paying For?

### Paid pays off when:
- You need watermark-free, HD clips
- You generate video regularly and want value
- Faster, higher-resolution output matters

### Free is enough when:
- The refreshing free credits cover your needs
- You're testing quality against rivals

## Tips to Optimize Hailuo Costs

1. **Use the free daily credits** before subscribing
2. **Keep prompts precise** to cut re-generations
3. **Match resolution to the platform** — don't overpay for HD you won't use
4. **Go annual** for the lower rate

## Bottom Line

Hailuo AI's pricing is one of the best values in text-to-video: genuinely useful free credits, a ~$10 Standard tier, and Pro plans for volume. If realism-per-dollar is your priority, it's a strong choice — start with the free credits to compare against Runway, Pika, and Veo.
