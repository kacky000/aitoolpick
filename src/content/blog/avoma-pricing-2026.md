# Avoma Pricing 2026: Plans, Tiers, and Value for Sales Teams

Avoma is an AI meeting assistant built for sales and customer-facing teams, combining transcription, note-taking, and conversation intelligence with CRM sync. In 2026 its pricing is tiered from a free plan up to revenue-intelligence packages. Here's the full breakdown.

## Pricing Plans at a Glance

| Plan | Monthly Cost (Annual) | Focus | Best For |
|------|----------------------|-------|----------|
| Basic (Free) | $0 | Transcription + notes | Individuals |
| Starter | ~$19 | Scheduling + notes | Solo professionals |
| Plus | ~$49 | Conversation intelligence | Growing teams |
| Business/Enterprise | ~$79+ | Revenue intelligence | Sales orgs |

## How Avoma's Pricing Works

Avoma layers features by tier: lower plans cover transcription, AI notes, and scheduling, while higher plans add conversation and revenue intelligence — analytics on talk patterns, deal risk, and coaching. Pricing is per user per month, with annual billing reducing the rate. That structure targets sales teams more than casual note-takers.

## Basic Plan (Free, $0)

The free tier provides AI meeting notes and transcription for individuals, enough to capture and review calls without paying.

**Best for:** Individuals testing AI notes.

## Starter (~$19/user/month billed annually)

Starter adds scheduling, more generous notes, and core assistant features for solo professionals who live in meetings.

**Best for:** Solo sales and success reps.

## Plus (~$49/user/month billed annually)

Plus introduces conversation intelligence — keyword tracking, topic analytics, and coaching signals — for teams that want insight, not just transcripts.

**Best for:** Growing sales and success teams.

## Business / Enterprise (~$79+/user/month)

Top tiers add revenue intelligence, deal and pipeline analytics, advanced admin, and integrations for organizations running structured sales motions.

**Best for:** Sales organizations and RevOps.

Avoma's differentiator is bundling meeting notes with conversation and revenue intelligence, positioning it against dedicated sales-analytics tools.

## Is Avoma Worth Paying For?

### Paid pays off when:
- You run a sales or success team needing coaching data
- CRM sync and deal analytics drive your workflow
- You want scheduling plus notes in one tool

### Free is enough when:
- You only need basic AI notes as an individual
- Conversation intelligence isn't relevant yet

## Tips to Optimize Avoma Costs

1. **Match the tier to your role** — don't pay for revenue intelligence you won't use
2. **Start free** to validate transcription quality
3. **Go annual** for the lower per-seat rate
4. **Consolidate tools** — Avoma can replace separate scheduler + notes apps

## Bottom Line

Avoma's pricing climbs from a free notes tier to ~$79+ revenue-intelligence plans, all per seat. It's strongest for sales teams that need analytics and coaching, not just transcripts. If you only want AI notes, start free or compare cheaper rivals; upgrade when intelligence features earn their cost.
