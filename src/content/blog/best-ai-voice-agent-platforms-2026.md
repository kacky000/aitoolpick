AI voice agents handle inbound customer calls, outbound sales, appointment scheduling, and support triage — without a human on the line. The category matured significantly in 2026, with several platforms offering sub-700ms latency and enterprise-grade compliance. Here are the best options and who each one serves.

## Quick Comparison

| Platform | Starting Price | Latency | Best For |
|----------|---------------|---------|----------|
| **Retell AI** | $0.07+/min | ~600 ms | Enterprise, support teams |
| **Vapi** | $0.05+/min* | ~500–700 ms | Developer teams, custom builds |
| **Bland AI** | $0.09/min | ~700 ms | SMBs, easy setup |
| **Synthflow** | From $29/month | Varies | Non-technical teams |
| **ElevenLabs Conversational** | Bundled | ~400 ms | Premium voice quality |

*Vapi's base price excludes STT, LLM, and TTS — real cost is $0.10–0.20/min in practice.

## 1. Retell AI — Best for Enterprise Teams

Retell AI is the most complete platform for business teams who want a production-ready AI phone agent without heavy developer involvement. It includes a visual workflow builder, pre-built templates for support and sales, and enterprise compliance features (HIPAA-ready configurations, structured dialog flows, audit logs).

At $0.07+/min with no hidden component costs, Retell's pricing is the most predictable in the category. For regulated industries — healthcare, fintech, legal — Retell is the default choice.

**Strengths:** Visual builder, fast deployment, enterprise compliance, transparent pricing.  
**Weakness:** Less flexibility than Vapi for custom voice products.

## 2. Vapi — Best for Developer Teams

Vapi gives developers maximum control over every component: speech-to-text provider, LLM, text-to-speech voice, turn-taking behavior. The flexibility makes it the strongest choice for bespoke voice products where off-the-shelf templates don't cut it.

In August 2026, Vapi retired its visual Workflows builder and moved to a Squads-based configuration model, which raised the technical barrier. Teams comfortable with API configuration and prompt engineering will unlock Vapi's full potential; others may find Retell more productive.

**Strengths:** Maximum customization, tunable latency (can reach under 500 ms), wide provider support.  
**Weakness:** Higher setup complexity, less predictable all-in pricing.

See our detailed [Vapi vs Retell AI comparison](/blog/vapi-vs-retell-ai-2026/) for a head-to-head breakdown.

## 3. Bland AI — Best for SMBs and Quick Deployment

Bland AI offers a simpler onboarding experience aimed at businesses without a dedicated technical team. Pricing is straightforward at $0.09/min. Latency (~700 ms) is slightly higher than Retell and Vapi, but acceptable for most business call use cases.

The platform covers core use cases — inbound support, outbound appointment booking — with less configuration overhead than Vapi.

**Strengths:** Simple setup, flat pricing, good for non-technical operators.  
**Weakness:** Fewer customization options, slightly higher latency.

## 4. Synthflow — Best for No-Code Teams

Synthflow is designed for operators who want to deploy AI voice agents through a visual interface with no code at all. Monthly plans start at $29 and scale with call volume. It's not the most powerful option, but it gets non-technical teams to a working phone agent faster than any other platform.

**Strengths:** No-code, accessible pricing, fast time-to-deploy.  
**Weakness:** Less suitable for complex, custom workflows.

## 5. ElevenLabs Conversational AI — Best for Voice Quality

ElevenLabs entered the voice agent space with its Conversational AI product, leveraging its market-leading text-to-speech quality. If voice naturalness and brand identity are the top priority — luxury brands, premium customer experiences — ElevenLabs delivers the most human-sounding agents in the category.

It's less focused on business workflow features than Retell or Vapi, but for voice-first use cases it's a serious option.

**Strengths:** Best-in-class voice quality, ultra-low latency (~400 ms).  
**Weakness:** Fewer workflow and compliance features than enterprise-focused platforms.

## How to Choose

| Your situation | Best pick |
|---------------|-----------|

| Custom voice product, dev team | Vapi |
| SMB, quick deployment | Bland AI |
| Non-technical team, no code | Synthflow |
