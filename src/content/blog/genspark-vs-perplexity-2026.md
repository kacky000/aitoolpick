Genspark and Perplexity both replace traditional search for research tasks — but they take fundamentally different approaches. Perplexity answers questions fast with web citations. Genspark executes multi-step agent tasks and produces structured Sparkpages. Here's how to pick the right one.

## Where Genspark Wins

**Sparkpage quality for deep research.** When you need a structured, multi-section document — not just an answer — Genspark's Sparkpages deliver. Ask "compare the top 5 project management tools for remote teams" and you get a formatted report with sections, comparison tables, and citations. Perplexity gives you a sourced paragraph.

**AI phone calls.** This is Genspark's killer differentiator. You can instruct it to call a business, verify pricing, check reservation availability, or gather real-world information. Perplexity has no equivalent. For research that requires going beyond the web, this is a meaningful edge.

**Autonomous multi-step execution.** Genspark's agents break complex tasks into subtasks and execute them in sequence. Perplexity's Deep Research (Pro) runs multiple searches but is less configurable and less "agentic" in the true sense.

## Where Perplexity Wins

**Speed for quick factual queries.** For "what's the current pricing on Notion?" or "summarize the latest developments in AI regulation," Perplexity returns a sourced, accurate answer in seconds. Genspark's agent overhead makes it slower for simple one-off questions.

**Academic and professional sources.** Perplexity Pro includes access to academic databases, medical literature, and finance sources. For research that needs to cite peer-reviewed studies or SEC filings, Perplexity is better equipped.

**API access for builders.** Perplexity's Sonar API lets developers embed real-time web search into their own applications. Genspark doesn't offer equivalent API access, which limits it to end-user research rather than application development.

**Lower paid tier entry point.** Perplexity Pro starts at $20/month vs Genspark Plus at $24.99/month. For users who just need the AI search features without phone calls and advanced agents, Perplexity is slightly cheaper.

## Use Case Match

**Choose Genspark when:**
- You need a structured research report, not just an answer
- You want to gather real-world information via AI phone calls
- You're running complex, multi-step research workflows
- You want high free tier value (chat and images free through 2026)

**Choose Perplexity when:**
- You want fast, sourced answers to specific questions
- You need academic or professional database access
- You're building applications with the Sonar API
- You want the lower-priced paid tier

**Use both when:**
Many researchers use Perplexity for rapid question-answering during initial exploration, then Genspark to produce polished Sparkpages for final deliverables. The workflows complement each other.

## The Bottom Line

Perplexity is the better tool for quick, factual, web-sourced answers. Genspark is the better tool for generating structured research documents and running autonomous tasks.

If your primary workflow is "I need to know the answer to X," Perplexity wins on speed. If your workflow is "I need a research deliverable on X," Genspark's Sparkpages are worth the extra $5/month.
