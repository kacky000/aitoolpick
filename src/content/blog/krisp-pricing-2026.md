# Krisp Pricing 2026: Free, Pro, and Business Plans Explained

Krisp started as an AI noise-cancellation app and has grown into a broader meeting assistant with transcription, notes, and call summaries — all running locally on your device. In 2026 it offers a free tier plus paid plans. Here's what each costs and includes.

## Pricing Plans at a Glance

| Plan | Monthly Cost (Annual) | Key Features | Best For |
|------|----------------------|--------------|----------|
| Free | $0 | Limited daily noise removal + AI notes | Light users |
| Pro | ~$8 | Unlimited noise cancellation, meeting notes | Individuals |
| Business | ~$15+ | Team admin, analytics, SSO | Teams |
| Enterprise | Custom | Advanced security, controls | Organizations |

## How Krisp's Pricing Works

Krisp's free tier caps noise cancellation to a daily minute allowance, while paid plans unlock unlimited use plus the AI meeting assistant (transcription, notes, summaries). Because processing runs on-device, it works across any conferencing app. Pricing is per user, with annual billing lowering the rate.

## Free Plan ($0)

The free tier gives a limited daily amount of AI noise and echo cancellation, plus basic meeting notes. It's a real way to hear the noise-removal quality before paying.

**Best for:** Occasional calls and testing.

## Pro Plan (~$8/month billed annually)

Pro removes the daily cap for unlimited noise cancellation and unlocks the full AI meeting assistant — transcripts, summaries, and action items across any app.

**Best for:** Remote workers on frequent calls.

## Business Plan (~$15+/user/month)

Business adds centralized administration, usage analytics, SSO, and team management for organizations standardizing on Krisp.

**Best for:** Teams and IT-managed deployments.

## Enterprise (Custom)

Enterprise layers advanced security, compliance, and controls under custom pricing.

**Best for:** Large or regulated organizations.

## Is Krisp Worth Paying For?

### Paid pays off when:
- You take calls from noisy environments daily
- You want unlimited noise removal plus meeting notes
- One tool across every conferencing app appeals

### Free is enough when:
- You have occasional, short calls
- The daily noise-removal cap covers you

## Tips to Optimize Krisp Costs

1. **Test the free daily minutes** to judge noise quality
2. **Go annual** for the lower monthly rate
3. **Use the bundled assistant** to replace a separate notes tool
4. **Pick Business only** when you need admin controls

## Bottom Line

Krisp's pricing is approachable: a free tier to sample noise removal, ~$8 Pro for unlimited use plus AI notes, and Business tiers for teams. If clear audio across any app is your priority — with meeting notes as a bonus — Krisp is strong value. Start free, then upgrade when the daily cap gets in your way.
