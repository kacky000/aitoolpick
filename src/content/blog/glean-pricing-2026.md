Glean's pricing isn't on their website. That's intentional — this is enterprise software, and the actual number you'll pay depends heavily on your organization's size, the features you need, and how hard you negotiate.

But based on publicly reported deployments and review data from 2026, the real-world range is clear enough to work with. Here's what you're likely looking at.

## Glean Pricing Overview

Glean doesn't publish list prices. Based on verified customer accounts and market reports:

| Deployment Size | Estimated Monthly Cost | Per-User Cost |
|-----------------|----------------------|---------------|
| Small (100–250 users) | ~$4,500–$12,000/month | $45–$75/user |
| Mid-market (250–700 users) | ~$15,000–$35,000/month | $45–$60/user |
| Enterprise (700–1,000+ users) | $300K–$500K+/year | $25–$40/user at scale |

One April 2026 deployment with approximately 200–230 users reported $45–$48/user/month. A company with 700 employees estimated $250,000/year — roughly $30/user/month at that scale. Volume discounts are real, but you have to reach substantial headcount before they kick in.

There is no free plan, no self-serve trial, and no entry-level tier. Glean is enterprise-only with a minimum seat count (typically 50+).

## What's Included in Glean's Pricing

Glean's core product is enterprise AI search — it connects to your company's tools (Slack, Confluence, Google Drive, Salesforce, GitHub, Zendesk, and 100+ others) and makes all that content searchable through a single AI interface.

**Core features included in all contracts:**
- Universal enterprise search across connected apps
- AI-generated summaries and answers citing sources
- Permission-respecting results (users only see content they have access to)
- Glean Chat (conversational AI assistant that pulls from company knowledge)
- Glean Agents (AI automation capabilities for repetitive tasks)
- Analytics dashboard for IT/admin

**What's typically extra or negotiated separately:**
- Custom integrations beyond the standard connector library
- Advanced analytics and workforce intelligence modules
- Dedicated customer success manager (sometimes included for larger contracts)
- Professional services / onboarding assistance

## Why There's No Published Pricing

Glean's market position requires enterprise pricing secrecy. They're competing against Microsoft Copilot (bundled with M365), which makes transparent price comparison difficult. Glean's argument is that their search quality and cross-tool knowledge graph justifies the premium — but that's a claim you can't evaluate without a proof-of-concept deployment.

The sales process typically looks like: outreach → discovery call → scoped POC (proof of concept, usually 2–4 weeks) → commercial proposal. Plan for a 1–3 month sales cycle before you're live.

## The Real Cost Calculation

For a 300-person company paying $50/user/month:
- **Monthly cost**: $15,000
- **Annual cost**: $180,000
- **Plus potential professional services**: $15,000–$25,000 for implementation

Glean claims users save 110 hours/year each through faster information retrieval. If your average employee costs $50/hour fully loaded, 110 hours = $5,500 in recovered time per user annually. For a 300-person company, that's $1.65M in theoretical productivity value against $180K in software cost — a compelling ROI argument on paper.

The real question is whether your team actually captures that productivity gain or just finds new ways to spend the recovered time.

## Who Should Consider Glean

**Glean makes financial sense when:**
- Your company has 300+ employees across multiple tools (Slack, Salesforce, Google Workspace, Confluence, GitHub, etc.)
- Knowledge workers spend significant time searching for information across systems
- You've validated this is a real productivity bottleneck, not a perception problem
- IT/security requires permission-respecting search (critical for compliance)

**Skip Glean if:**
- You're under 200 people — the cost-per-user is hard to justify at small scale
- Your team primarily lives in one ecosystem (M365 → use Copilot; Google Workspace → Gemini for Workspace)
- You want a self-serve trial before committing (doesn't exist)
- Budget under $100K/year for productivity software tools

## Negotiation Tips

Glean salespeople will negotiate. Points where companies typically get movement:
- **Annual prepay**: Full year upfront typically gets 10–15% discount
- **Multi-year commitment**: 2–3 year contracts often unlock larger discounts
- **POC scope**: Push for a real proof-of-concept with actual data before signing
- **Professional services**: Sometimes included for larger contracts
- **User minimums**: If your headcount is under the default minimum, ask about phased rollout pricing

Don't sign on the first proposal. The published "standard" pricing is the starting point, not the endpoint.

## The Bottom Line

Glean is premium-priced enterprise software — $45–$75/user/month puts it in a different category than most productivity tools. For large organizations with genuine cross-tool knowledge fragmentation, it can pay for itself. For companies under 200 people, or those where teams primarily live in a single ecosystem, the cost is hard to justify.

The lack of a self-serve trial is the biggest friction point. If you can't get a scoped POC with your real data before buying, that's a risk signal.
