# Recraft Pricing 2026: Free, Basic, Advanced, and Pro Plans Breakdown

Recraft is an AI design tool known for generating not just images but editable vectors, icons, and brand-consistent sets. In 2026 its pricing runs on a credit system. Here's the full breakdown of what each tier costs and how many designs you actually get.

## Pricing Plans at a Glance

| Plan | Monthly Cost (Annual) | Daily/Monthly Credits | Best For |
|------|----------------------|----------------------|----------|
| Free | $0 | ~50 credits/day | Casual use, testing |
| Basic | ~$12 | ~1,000 credits/mo | Individual creators |
| Advanced | ~$33 | ~5,000 credits/mo | Regular design work |
| Pro | ~$48+ | ~10,000+ credits/mo | Professionals/teams |

## How Credits Work

Recraft charges credits per generation. A raster image, a vector, or an icon each costs a set number of credits, and higher-resolution or batch outputs cost more. The free plan refills daily credits (no rollover); paid plans give a larger monthly pool plus commercial rights.

## Free Plan ($0)

The free tier refills a small batch of credits daily and lets you try image and vector generation. Outputs are generally for non-commercial use and public by default, but it's a real way to evaluate Recraft's standout vector output.

**Best for:** Trying the tool and casual, non-commercial designs.

## Basic Plan (~$12/month billed annually)

Basic adds a monthly credit pool, commercial usage rights, private generations, and access to more features. For an individual creator producing icons, illustrations, or brand assets, it's the natural entry tier.

**Best for:** Solo designers and creators with steady output.

## Advanced Plan (~$33/month billed annually)

Advanced roughly multiplies your credit pool and unlocks higher limits and priority. It suits designers and small teams generating assets frequently for client or product work.

**Best for:** Working designers and small studios.

## Pro Plan (~$48+/month billed annually)

Pro is the high-volume tier with the largest credit pool, top priority, and full feature access — built for professionals whose workflow depends on generating design assets at scale.

**Best for:** Agencies and full-time designers.

## How Recraft Compares on Price

| Tool | Entry Paid Price | Strength |
|------|-----------------|----------|
| Recraft Basic | ~$12/mo | Editable vectors + brand sets |
| Midjourney Basic | ~$10/mo | Highest raster aesthetics |
| Ideogram | ~$8/mo | Text rendering in images |
| Leonardo AI Apprentice | ~$12/mo | Game/art assets, control |

Recraft's differentiator is vectors and design consistency: it generates SVGs and brand-styled sets that stay editable, where most rivals output raster images only.

## Is Recraft Worth Paying For?

### Paid pays off when:
- You need editable vectors, icons, or logos — not just images
- You require commercial rights and private generations
- Brand-consistent asset sets save you production time

### Free is enough when:
- You generate designs casually and non-commercially
- The daily credit refill covers your needs

## Tips to Optimize Recraft Costs

1. **Use vectors where they matter** — editability beats regenerating rasters
2. **Batch carefully** — more outputs per prompt means more credits spent
3. **Go annual** — the discount versus monthly is meaningful
4. **Start free** — the daily credits show how fast you'd hit a paid cap

## Bottom Line

Recraft earns its price on vectors and brand consistency, not raw image quality. Basic at ~$12/month annually is a strong entry for designers who need editable, commercial-ready assets; Advanced and Pro scale for professionals. Test the free daily credits to gauge your real usage first.
