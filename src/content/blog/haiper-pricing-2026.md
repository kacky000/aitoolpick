# Haiper Pricing 2026: Free, Pro, and Credit System Explained

Haiper is an AI video generator that turns text and images into short clips, competing in the fast-moving text-to-video space. In 2026 it runs on a tiered credit system with a free entry point. Here's what each plan costs and how many videos you actually get.

## Pricing Plans at a Glance

| Plan | Monthly Cost (Annual) | Credits | Best For |
|------|----------------------|---------|----------|
| Free | $0 | Limited monthly credits, watermark | Testing |
| Pro | ~$24 | Larger pool, no watermark | Creators |
| Unlimited/Team | ~$60+ | High or unlimited generation | Heavy users |

## How Haiper's Credits Work

Each video generation consumes credits based on length, resolution, and mode. The free plan gives a small monthly allotment with watermarked output; paid tiers expand the pool, remove watermarks, unlock higher resolution, and add faster generation. Annual billing lowers the effective monthly rate.

## Free Plan ($0)

The free tier lets you generate a limited number of watermarked clips per month at standard quality. It's a real way to test Haiper's motion quality and prompt handling before paying.

**Best for:** Trying text-to-video and casual experiments.

## Pro Plan (~$24/month billed annually)

Pro removes watermarks, raises the credit pool, and unlocks higher resolution and priority generation. For a content creator making regular short clips, it's the practical working tier.

**Best for:** Creators producing social and marketing video.

## Unlimited / Team (~$60+/month)

Higher tiers offer very large or unlimited generation, top priority, and team features — aimed at studios and high-volume producers.

**Best for:** Agencies and power users.

## How Haiper Compares on Price

| Tool | Entry Paid Price | Strength |
|------|-----------------|----------|
| Haiper Pro | ~$24/mo | Accessible text-to-video |
| Runway | ~$15/mo | Pro editing + video tools |
| Pika | ~$10/mo | Fast, fun short clips |
| Luma AI | ~$10/mo | Realistic motion |

Haiper's differentiator is an approachable interface and steady motion quality at a mainstream creator price.

## Is Haiper Worth Paying For?

### Paid pays off when:
- You need watermark-free, higher-resolution clips
- You generate video regularly for content
- Faster generation saves you real time

### Free is enough when:
- You're testing prompts and motion quality
- Watermarks and lower limits don't block you

## Tips to Optimize Haiper Costs

1. **Draft on free, finalize on paid** to avoid wasting credits
2. **Keep clips short** — length drives credit cost
3. **Refine prompts before generating** to reduce re-rolls
4. **Go annual** for the lower effective rate

## Bottom Line

Haiper's pricing is creator-friendly: a free tier to test, Pro around $24/month annually for watermark-free work, and higher tiers for volume. If you make short-form video regularly, Pro is the sweet spot — but start free to judge quality against Runway, Pika, and Luma first.
