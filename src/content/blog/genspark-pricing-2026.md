Genspark uses a credit-based pricing model with four tiers: Free, Plus, Pro, and Team. The free tier is unusually generous — especially with AI chat and image generation costing zero credits through December 2026. Here's what each plan actually includes.

## Genspark Plans at a Glance

| Plan | Price | Credits/Day | Best For |
|------|-------|-------------|---------|
| **Free** | $0 | 100/day | Casual research, evaluation |
| **Plus** | $24.99/month | Higher limit | Regular research users |
| **Pro** | $249.99/month | Unlimited (heavy tasks) | Power users, agencies |
| **Team** | $30/seat/month | Team pooling | Small teams |

*Annual billing reduces Plus to ~$19.99/month and Pro to ~$199.99/month.*

*Current pricing valid through December 31, 2026 (promotional period).*

## Free Plan

**Cost:** $0 — no credit card required.

**What you get:**
- 100 credits per day
- AI chat (zero credits through Dec 31, 2026)
- Image generation (zero credits through Dec 31, 2026)
- Sparkpage generation (credits apply)
- Basic multi-step research tasks

**What you miss:**
- Higher credit limits for agentic tasks
- AI phone call credits
- Team collaboration features
- Priority processing

The free tier is a real evaluation tool, not a stripped-down demo. With AI chat and image gen free through the end of 2026, most light to moderate users can run Genspark for research, summaries, and image creation without touching their 100 daily credits.

## Plus Plan: $24.99/Month

**Best for:** Individuals who regularly use Genspark for research and want more agentic task capacity.

**What you get over free:**
- Significantly higher daily credit allowance
- Access to AI phone calls (credit-based)
- Priority queue for complex tasks
- ~$20/month when billed annually

The Plus plan is where most active users land. If you're using Genspark daily for competitive research, market analysis, or Sparkpage generation, the free credit cap becomes a bottleneck within a few hours of work.

## Pro Plan: $249.99/Month

**Best for:** Agencies, power researchers, and teams with heavy autonomous agent workflows.

At $249.99/month (or ~$199.99 annual), Pro is priced for organizations where agentic research replaces significant human labor. If you're running dozens of complex research tasks per day — competitive monitoring, industry analysis, AI phone call outreach at scale — the credit economics start to make sense.

For individual users, this plan is almost certainly overkill. The Plus plan handles the vast majority of personal and small-business research workloads.

## Team Plan: $30/Seat/Month

**Best for:** Teams that want to collaborate on shared research and Sparkpages.

The Team plan runs at $30 per seat per month. Features include:
- Shared workspaces
- Collaborative Sparkpage editing
- Team-level credit pooling
- Admin controls and usage monitoring

For a 5-person team, that's $150/month — more expensive than the Plus plan but structured for coordination rather than solo use.

## How Credits Work

Every action on Genspark costs credits, except AI chat and image generation (which are free through December 31, 2026). Credit-consuming actions include:

- **Multi-step agent research**: 5–20 credits depending on task complexity
- **Sparkpage generation**: Varies by length and source count
- **AI phone calls**: Higher credit cost (exact amount varies by call duration)
- **Video generation**: Among the most credit-intensive features

The credit system means the advertised plan price isn't the only cost variable — heavy users will burn through credits faster than light users at the same plan tier.

## Is Genspark Worth It?

**Free plan:** Worth trying immediately. No credit card, real functionality, especially through 2026 while chat and images are free.

**Plus at $24.99/month:** Worth it if you're actively using Genspark for research several times per week and hitting the free credit ceiling.

**Pro at $249.99/month:** Narrow use case. Only justified for agencies or power users running continuous agentic workflows.

**Team at $30/seat:** Compare against individual Plus plans for your team size. For teams under 5, individual Plus plans may be cheaper unless shared collaboration is a priority.
