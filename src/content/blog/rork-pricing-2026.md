Rork is the AI mobile app builder that converts plain-English prompts into deployable iOS and Android apps using React Native. Unlike Bolt or Lovable (which target web apps), Rork is built exclusively for native mobile. Here's exactly what each plan costs and who it's for.

## Rork Pricing Plans at a Glance

| Plan | Price | AI Messages | Best For |
|------|-------|-------------|----------|
| **Free** | $0/month | 35 credits/month, 5/day | Testing, simple prototypes |
| **Junior** | $25/month | 100 messages | Idea validation, demos |
| **Senior** | $100/month | ~500 messages | Full MVP development |
| **Scale 1K** | $200/month | 1,000 messages | Production iteration |
| **Rork Max** | $200/month | Unlimited (native Swift) | Serious iOS-first products |

## Free Plan

The free tier gives you 35 credits per month with a 5-per-day cap. It's enough to test Rork's prompt-to-app pipeline and generate a simple prototype, but serious iterating — the kind you need to ship a real product — will burn through credits fast. Treat it as a trial, not a working plan.

## Junior ($25/month)

100 AI messages per month. This is the entry point for founders who want to validate an app concept without committing to a heavy build. For simple apps with one or two core screens, it can get you to a demo-ready state. Don't expect to build and refine a complete product on 100 messages.

## Senior ($100/month)

The Senior plan is Rork's sweet spot for active development. Roughly 500 AI messages per month supports real iteration: refining screens, fixing bugs, adding features. If you're building an MVP you plan to show investors or launch to early users, this is the minimum viable plan.

## Scale 1K ($200/month)

1,000 messages per month for teams doing high-volume iteration or running multiple projects. At this tier, the per-message cost drops meaningfully compared to the Junior plan.

## Rork Max ($200/month)

Rork Max is a separate premium product that switches from React Native to **native Swift**. This matters if you want an app that feels completely native on iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, Apple TV, Vision Pro, or iMessage. Rork Max also includes 2-click App Store publishing. Same price as Scale 1K but targets a different use case: iOS-first products that need the full Apple ecosystem.

## Hidden Costs to Watch

Rork AI is free to try but not free to build seriously. Credit consumption varies by prompt complexity — simple edits use 1–2 credits, full feature builds can use 5–10. Complex apps requiring many iterations will push you toward higher tiers faster than expected.

## Is Rork Worth It?

Compared to hiring a mobile developer ($80–150/hour), even the Senior plan at $100/month is a fraction of the cost if Rork can get you to a working prototype. Reviewers consistently praise how quickly the first working build appears. The caveat: once your app grows complex, you may hit the edges of what prompt-driven development can handle.
