# Hailuo AI Pricing 2026: Complete Breakdown

Understanding **Hailuo AI pricing** is the first step to figuring out whether MiniMax's fast-growing video generator fits your budget and workflow. Hailuo AI has become one of the most talked-about text-to-video tools of 2026 thanks to its sharp motion, cinematic prompts, and an unusually generous free tier. In this complete breakdown, we walk through every plan, explain how the credit system actually works, show who each tier suits, and compare Hailuo against the biggest rivals so you can pick the right subscription.

## What Is Hailuo AI?

Hailuo AI is an AI video generation platform built by MiniMax, a Chinese AI lab known for its powerful multimodal models. It lets you create short cinematic clips from a text prompt (text-to-video) or animate a still picture into a moving scene (image-to-video). The models are strong at natural camera movement, character consistency, and stylized visuals, which is why creators use Hailuo for social clips, ads, music visuals, and storyboards.

Most generations produce clips of roughly 5 to 10 seconds at a time. You can guide the output with prompt directions, reference images, and camera-movement controls. Because rendering video is compute-heavy, Hailuo AI charges through a **credit system** rather than a flat "unlimited" model on lower tiers, so knowing how credits translate into finished videos matters a lot.

## Free vs Paid Tiers

Hailuo AI offers a genuinely usable **Free plan** that refreshes a small batch of credits every day. It's ideal for testing prompts, learning the interface, and producing the occasional clip without paying anything. The trade-offs: outputs may carry a watermark, queue priority is lower, resolution is capped, and commercial-use rights are limited.

Paid tiers remove the watermark, unlock higher resolution, add faster (priority) rendering, and grant commercial licensing. The more you pay, the more monthly credits you get and the more concurrent generations you can run. The top tier moves toward effectively unlimited or high-volume "relaxed" generation for professionals and teams.

## How Hailuo Credits Work

Credits are the currency you spend each time you generate a video. The cost per generation depends on three main factors:

- **Clip length** — longer videos consume more credits.
- **Resolution & quality** — higher resolution (e.g., 1080p) costs more than draft quality.
- **Model & mode** — advanced models, image-to-video, and priority ("fast") rendering typically cost more than standard text-to-video.

As a rough guide, a single standard clip might cost around 10–30 credits, so a ~1,000-credit Standard plan yields roughly 80–100 finished videos per month depending on your settings. Free-tier daily credits reset every 24 hours and usually don't roll over, so use them or lose them. Paid credits generally last the full billing cycle. If you burn through your allotment, you can either wait for the next cycle or upgrade to a higher tier.

## Who Each Tier Suits

- **Free** — Perfect if you're curious, want to compare Hailuo against other tools, or only need a handful of clips per day. Great for students and hobbyists.
- **Standard (~$10/mo)** — The sweet spot for solo creators posting to TikTok, Reels, or Shorts a few times a week. Watermark-free, commercial rights, and enough credits for consistent output.
- **Pro (~$25/mo)** — Built for freelancers, small marketing teams, and content agencies producing dozens of clips weekly. Faster queues and larger credit pools keep production moving.
- **Unlimited / Enterprise (~$70+/mo)** — For agencies, studios, and high-volume teams that need relaxed or effectively unlimited generation, top resolution, and multiple concurrent renders.

- **vs Google Veo** — Veo delivers premium, high-fidelity results and longer clips, but it's positioned as a higher-cost, enterprise-leaning option. Hailuo is far more accessible for individuals. Review the [Google Veo Pricing](/blog/google-veo-pricing-2026) guide.

In short, Hailuo AI competes hardest on affordability and its friendly free tier, while Runway and Veo lean toward pro-grade features at higher price points, and Luma sits in a similar mid-tier lane.
