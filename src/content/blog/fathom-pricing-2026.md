# Fathom Pricing 2026: Complete Breakdown

If you sit in a lot of meetings, **Fathom pricing** is one of the friendliest stories in the AI notetaker space in 2026 — because its free plan is unusually generous. Fathom records, transcribes, and summarizes your calls, then generates action items and lets you search across everything. Unlike many rivals that cap free minutes hard, Fathom offers unlimited recording and summaries at no cost, reserving paid tiers for advanced AI, team features, and CRM automation. This guide breaks down every plan and who each fits.

## What Is Fathom?

Fathom is an AI meeting assistant that joins your Zoom, Google Meet, or Microsoft Teams calls to capture and summarize them automatically. Its focus is doing the free basics extremely well. Key features include:

- **Automatic recording and transcription** of your video meetings.
- **AI summaries and action items** generated right after each call.
- **Highlights and clips** you can share instead of forwarding whole recordings.
- **CRM and app integrations** (on paid tiers) to push notes into your workflow.

Because the free tier covers the core notetaking most people need, Fathom competes by making paid plans about depth and teamwork rather than basic access.

## Free vs Paid: What Actually Changes

The free plan is remarkably complete — you can record, transcribe, and summarize meetings without limits. What paid tiers add is **advanced AI, team collaboration, and workflow automation**, not basic notetaking.

**On the Free plan you get:**

- Unlimited meeting recording, transcripts, and AI summaries.
- Action items and shareable highlights.
- Enough for most individuals to replace manual note-taking entirely.

**Upgrading to Premium unlocks:**

- More advanced AI, including deeper "ask anything" queries across your meetings.
- Additional integrations and automation.
- Higher ceilings for heavy daily meeting use.

**Team Edition adds:**

- A shared meeting library so teammates can search each other's calls.
- Team analytics and admin controls.
- Features tuned for sales teams and collaborative workflows.

The practical takeaway: many users never need to pay Fathom at all. You upgrade when you want advanced AI queries, or when a team needs shared libraries and analytics.

## Who Each Tier Is For

**Free** — Individuals, freelancers, and anyone who wants reliable AI notes without a subscription. For most solo users this is genuinely enough.

**Premium (~$19/mo)** — Power users with heavy meeting loads who want the most advanced AI features and integrations.

**Team Edition (~$29/user/mo)** — Sales and collaborative teams that need shared meeting libraries, analytics, and admin controls.

**Enterprise** — Organizations requiring SSO, security governance, and dedicated support.

## Should You Ever Pay?

Because the free plan already covers unlimited recording, transcription, and summaries, the honest answer for many individuals is no. You start considering Premium when you find yourself wanting to interrogate past meetings — asking questions across weeks of calls — or when advanced integrations would save real time. The jump to Team Edition is about people, not features: the moment two or more colleagues need to search each other's meeting libraries and managers want analytics, the shared workspace justifies the per-seat cost. Until one of those triggers appears, staying on Free is the rational choice.
