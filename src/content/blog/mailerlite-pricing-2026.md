MailerLite has built a reputation as the best-value email marketing platform for creators, bloggers, and small businesses. Its free tier is genuinely competitive — but the details matter when you're deciding whether to upgrade.

Here's a complete breakdown of MailerLite's pricing in 2026.

## MailerLite Pricing Plans 2026

MailerLite pricing scales with your subscriber count. Here are the base prices at 1,000 subscribers:

| Plan | Price (1k subs) | Emails/Month | Users |
|------|----------------|-------------|-------|
| **Free** | $0 | 12,000 | 1 |
| **Growing Business** | $9/month | Unlimited | 3 |
| **Advanced** | $18/month | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| **Enterprise** | Custom | Unlimited | Unlimited |

**Note**: The free plan caps at 1,000 subscribers and 12,000 emails/month. Once you exceed 1,000 subscribers, you must upgrade.

## What's Included in Each Plan

### Free Plan (0–1,000 subscribers)
MailerLite's free tier is genuinely useful, not a stripped-down teaser:
- Drag-and-drop email editor
- Email automation (basic — 1 trigger per automation)
- 1 landing page
- Signup forms and pop-ups
- Campaign analytics (open rates, click rates, unsubscribes)
- 24/7 email support (first 30 days only)

**Limitations:**
- MailerLite branding in email footer
- No custom HTML editor
- No newsletter monetization
- No A/B split testing
- 1 user only
- 12,000 email sends/month cap

For early-stage creators with under 1,000 subscribers, the free plan is genuinely viable.

### Growing Business ($9/month at 1k subscribers)
The most popular upgrade tier:
- Everything in Free, plus:
- **Remove MailerLite branding**
- **Unlimited email sends**
- **Auto-resend campaigns** to non-openers
- **A/B split testing** for subject lines and content
- **Custom HTML editor** for full code control
- **Unlimited landing pages**
- **Newsletter monetization** (sell paid subscriptions)
- **Promotion pop-ups** with countdown timers
- **3 users**
- **24/7 live chat support**

The removal of branding alone is often worth the upgrade for professional senders.

### Advanced ($18/month at 1k subscribers)
For growing teams that need advanced automation:
- Everything in Growing Business, plus:
- **Multiple automations** with complex branching
- **Smart sending** (AI-optimized send time per recipient)
- **Facebook Custom Audiences** integration
- **Preference center** (subscribers manage their own preferences)
- **Unlimited users**
- **Dedicated account manager** (large accounts)
- **Custom newsletter templates** with advanced designer

### Enterprise (Custom pricing)
For accounts over 100,000 subscribers or with specific compliance needs:
- Everything in Advanced, plus:
- Dedicated infrastructure
- Custom deliverability support
- SSO and advanced security
- Custom contract terms

## Key Features Worth Paying For

**Newsletter monetization** (Growing Business+): If you're planning to offer paid newsletter subscriptions, MailerLite handles this natively — no need for a separate tool like Substack or Beehiiv. Stripe integration handles billing.

**Smart sending** (Advanced): AI-optimized delivery time per subscriber. Worth testing if you have over 5,000 engaged subscribers.

**Multiple automations** (Advanced): The free and Growing plans limit automation complexity. If you want sequences like welcome → engagement check → purchase offer → win-back, you need Advanced.

## When to Upgrade from Free to Growing Business

Upgrade when any of these apply:
- You have more than 1,000 subscribers
- The MailerLite branding in footers looks unprofessional for your brand
- You want A/B testing for subject lines
- You're sending promotional campaigns (not just newsletters)
- You need more than 12,000 sends/month

## Is MailerLite Worth It?

**Yes, if:**
- You're a creator, blogger, or small business owner
- You want a clean interface that's easy to learn
- Email marketing is important but not your core business
- You want the best value below 50,000 subscribers

**Consider alternatives if:**
- You need advanced behavioral automation (use ActiveCampaign)
- You're newsletter-first and want native discovery (use Beehiiv)
- You need deep e-commerce integration (use Klaviyo)

## Free Trial

MailerLite offers a **30-day free trial** of the Advanced plan with no credit card required. After 30 days, you can continue on the free plan or choose a paid tier.

## Bottom Line

MailerLite's pricing is among the most straightforward and affordable in the email marketing space. The free plan is legitimate for early creators; the Growing Business plan at $9/month is excellent value once you exceed 1,000 subscribers. Advanced at $18/month is worth it if you need complex automation or unlimited team members.
