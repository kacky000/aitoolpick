# Wan AI Pricing 2026: Complete Breakdown

Among the flood of AI video generators, **Wan AI pricing** stands out in 2026 for an unusual reason: the core models are open source. Wan (from Alibaba's Tongyi lab) releases open-weight text-to-video and image-to-video models that anyone can run, while hosted access is offered through credits on Alibaba's cloud and third-party platforms. That split — free to self-host, pay-per-generation in the cloud — makes Wan one of the more flexible options for creators and developers. This guide breaks down every path and who it fits.

## What Is Wan AI?

Wan (Wan2.x / Tongyi Wanxiang) is a family of AI video generation models from Alibaba. It converts text prompts or still images into short video clips and has become popular partly because the weights are openly available. Key features include:

- **Text-to-video and image-to-video** generation of short clips.
- **Open-weight models** you can download and run on your own GPU.
- **Hosted access** through Alibaba Cloud's Model Studio and various third-party sites, billed by credits.
- **Strong motion and prompt adherence** that made it a favorite in the open-source video community.

Because you can either self-host for free or buy hosted credits, your real cost depends entirely on which route you choose.

## Free vs Paid: Where the Money Really Goes

The models themselves are free; you pay for **compute** — either your own hardware or hosted credits.

**Self-hosting (free software) means:**

- No per-clip fees; you run the open weights locally or on rented GPUs.
- Full control and privacy, ideal for high-volume or commercial pipelines.
- A technical setup cost — you need a capable GPU and some configuration.

**Hosted access (credits) means:**

- No hardware needed; generate from a browser.
- You pay credits per clip, and longer/higher-resolution videos cost more.
- Free trial credits on some platforms let you test before buying.

The practical takeaway: if you have the hardware and volume, self-hosting Wan is unbeatable on marginal cost. If you just want a few clips, hosted credits are simpler and cheaper up front.

## Who Each Route Is For

**Self-hosted** — Developers, studios, and power users who generate lots of video, need privacy, or want to build Wan into a product without per-clip fees.

**Free hosted tier** — Creators sampling Wan's quality against other generators before spending anything.

**Credit packs / subscriptions** — Creators and marketers without a GPU who want reliable hosted generation and predictable top-ups.
