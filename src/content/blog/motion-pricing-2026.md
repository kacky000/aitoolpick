# Motion Pricing 2026: Individual, Team, and Enterprise Plans Breakdown

Motion combines an AI calendar, task manager, and project manager that auto-schedules your day around deadlines and priorities. In 2026 it's positioned as a premium productivity tool. Here's exactly what it costs and whether the auto-scheduling earns its price.

## Pricing Plans at a Glance

| Plan | Monthly Cost (Annual) | Monthly Billing | Best For |
|------|----------------------|-----------------|----------|
| Individual | ~$19 | ~$34 | Solo professionals |
| Team / Business | ~$12/user | ~$20/user | Teams (3+) |
| Enterprise | Custom | Custom | Larger organizations |

Note: Motion pushes annual billing hard — the monthly-billed rate is significantly higher, so the "$19" figure assumes an annual commitment.

## What Motion Actually Does

Motion's core feature is automatic scheduling. You add tasks with durations, deadlines, and priorities, and its AI arranges them into your calendar around meetings — rescheduling automatically when things change. You're paying for that scheduling engine plus project management on top.

## Individual Plan (~$19/month billed annually)

The Individual plan gives one user the full AI calendar, task manager, and project tools. Billed monthly it's around $34, which is why nearly everyone chooses annual.

**Best for:** Freelancers, founders, and professionals juggling many deadlines.

## Team / Business Plan (~$12/user/month billed annually)

Team pricing is per-user and lower than Individual, adding shared projects, team calendars, workload views, and collaboration. It's designed for small teams that want auto-scheduling across everyone's work.

**Best for:** Startups and teams coordinating projects and capacity.

## Enterprise Plan (Custom)

Enterprise adds SSO, advanced admin controls, security features, and dedicated support with custom pricing.

## How Motion Compares on Price

| Tool | Entry Price | Focus |
|------|------------|-------|
| Motion Individual | ~$19/mo (annual) | AI auto-scheduling + PM |
| Reclaim.ai | ~$8/mo | AI calendar scheduling |
| Sunsama | ~$16/mo | Daily planning ritual |
| Notion | ~$10/mo | Docs + databases + tasks |

Motion is pricier than most because it bundles calendar AI *and* project management. Reclaim does scheduling for less; Sunsama does calm daily planning; Notion does flexible docs — Motion tries to do all three with automation.

## Is Motion Worth It?

### It's worth it when:
- You have more tasks than time and constant reprioritization
- Manual calendar Tetris eats real hours each week
- You want tasks and projects to auto-fit your schedule

### Look elsewhere when:
- You just need calendar scheduling (Reclaim is cheaper)
- You prefer a manual, intentional planning ritual (Sunsama)
- The monthly-billed price feels steep and you won't commit annually

## Tips to Optimize Motion Costs

1. **Commit annually** — monthly billing is roughly 75% more expensive
2. **Use Team pricing at 3+ seats** — per-user rate beats Individual
3. **Trial it fully** — auto-scheduling either clicks with your brain or doesn't
4. **Migrate all tasks in** — Motion only optimizes what it can see

## Bottom Line

Motion is a premium bet on automation: at ~$19/month annually it costs more than single-purpose tools, but it replaces a calendar app, a to-do list, and a project manager with one auto-scheduling system. If deadline juggling is your daily pain, it can pay for itself; if you just need scheduling, cheaper tools do the job.
