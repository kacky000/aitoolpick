# Supermaven Pricing 2026: Complete Breakdown

If raw autocomplete speed is what you care about, **Supermaven pricing** deserves a close look in 2026. Supermaven built its reputation on the fastest, longest-context code completion available — suggestions that appear almost instantly and understand huge swaths of your codebase. After the team joined Cursor, the standalone extension continued for developers who want elite completion inside their existing editor. This guide breaks down every tier, what the paid plan actually buys, and who should pay.

## What Is Supermaven?

Supermaven is an AI code-completion extension for VS Code, JetBrains, and Neovim. Its focus is narrow and deep: predict your next lines of code faster and with more context than anything else. Standout features include:

- **A very large context window** so completions reflect your whole project, not just the open file.
- **Extremely low latency** — suggestions render almost as fast as you type.
- **A built-in chat** for questions and edits alongside completion.
- **Editor-native integration** that layers onto the IDE you already use.

Because Supermaven concentrates on completion quality and speed rather than a full agent workflow, it appeals to developers who find heavier AI IDEs distracting.

## Free vs Paid: What Actually Changes

The Free tier is genuinely usable — you still get the signature speed and long context that made Supermaven famous. What Pro changes is **model quality and ceiling**, not access to the feature set.

**On the Free plan you get:**

- Fast, low-latency completion across your files.
- A large context window that most rivals reserve for paid tiers.
- Basic chat for quick questions.

**Upgrading to Pro unlocks:**

- The higher-quality completion model for smarter, longer suggestions.
- An even larger context window for big monorepos.
- A more capable chat assistant for edits and explanations.

**Team adds:**

- Pro across every seat with unified billing and management.

The practical takeaway: if you only want fast completion and code in small projects, Free may be enough. If you work in large codebases and want the smartest suggestions all day, Pro is a low-cost upgrade.

## Who Each Tier Is For

**Free** — Students, hobbyists, and developers evaluating whether Supermaven's speed fits their flow.

**Pro (~$10/mo)** — The sweet spot for professional solo developers who want top-tier completion in a big codebase without paying for a full agentic IDE.

**Team (~$10/user/mo)** — Small teams that want everyone on the same fast-completion setup with simple billing.

## Is Pro Worth It?

The upgrade math is simple. If you work in a small codebase and only want fast suggestions, the Free tier's model and context are already excellent, and paying may not change your day much. But in a large monorepo, the Pro model's larger context means suggestions understand more of your project, which translates into fewer wrong completions and less time correcting them. At roughly $10/mo, one or two saved hours a month more than covers the cost — which is why most professional developers who trial Free end up on Pro within a few weeks.
